NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The New Mexico Department of Health (Department) will hold a public hearing on the proposed adoption of a new 
rule, 7.35.4 NMAC, and the proposed amendment of existing rule section 7.35.2.7 NMAC,  concerning the New 
Mexico Medical Psilocybin Program (“Program”). The hearing will be held on Friday, November 20, 2026 at 9:00 
a.m. in the Harold Runnels Building auditorium, located at 1190 St. Francis Drive, Santa Fe, New Mexico.  The 
hearing will also be broadcast via a live web-based video conference, and via telephone. Members of the public who 
wish to submit public comment regarding the proposed rule will be able to do so in person at the hearing, via video 
conference, or via telephone during the hearing, and by submitting written comment. 
 
The proposed rule 7.35.4 NMAC would adopt standards for the Medical Psilocybin Treatment Equity Fund.  The 
rule includes, but is not limited to, the following: 
 
• Eligibility standards and submittal requirements for qualified New Mexico patients  
• Covered medical services 
• Claim and invoice submittal requirements 
• Reimbursement standards 
• Record retention and audit requirements 
• Permitted travel expenses 
• Program monitoring  
• Grounds for denial, payment holds, and suspensions 
• Recovery of overpayments 
• Process for review of adverse determinations 
 
The proposed amendment of 7.35.2.7 NMAC would adopt revisions to the Definitions of that rule section, which 
would be incorporated by reference at 7.35.4.7 NMAC. 
 
The purpose of the proposed rule and rule amendment is to implement the Medical Psilocybin Act, sections 26-2D-1 
through -11, NMSA 1978. 
 
The legal authority authorizing the adoption of the rule and rule amendment by the Department is the Department of 
Health Act, subsection E of section 9-7-6 NMSA 1978, which authorizes the secretary of the department of health to 
“...make and adopt such reasonable and procedural rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the duties 
of the department and its divisions”; and the Medical Psilocybin Act, at section 26-2D-11(A), NMSA 1978, which 
requires the Department to administer the Medical Psilocybin Treatment Equity Fund, and at section 26-2D-7(A)(7), 
NMSA 1978, which requires the Department to establish “other requirements, restrictions and limitations 
promulgated by the department to ensure an efficacious program.” 
 
A free copy of the full text of the proposed rule and proposed rule amendment can be obtained online from the New 
Mexico Department of Health’s website at http://nmhealth.org/about/asd/cmo/rules/or by contacting the Department 
using the contact information below. 
 
The public hearing will be conducted to receive public comment on the  proposed rule and proposed rule 
amendment.  Any interested member of the public may attend the hearing and may submit data, views, or arguments 
on the proposed rule either orally or in writing during the hearing.
