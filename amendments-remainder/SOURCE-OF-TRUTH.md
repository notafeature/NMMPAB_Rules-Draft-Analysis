## 1. Scope, and the line between the two drafts

This document covers the twenty-four sections of 7.35.3 NMAC that the practicum amendment draft in
`amendments/` does not reach: 7.35.3.1 through .13, .15, .16, .17, and .21 through .28. The practicum draft
covers 7.35.3.14, 7.35.3.18, 7.35.3.19, Paragraph (5) of Subsection H of 7.35.3.20, and a proposed 7.35.3.29.
**Nothing here reopens the practicum draft.** Its `amendments/SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md` governs everything inside its
scope, including the hours, the permit renaming, and the two versions of the first practicum stage. Addendum B
of this document records every dependency between the two scopes, in both directions.

Twenty-four provisions are amended across nineteen sections. Every change is a mechanical correction, a
conforming amendment between two provisions of the same rule, or a restoration of wording the July 9, 2026
draft carried. No change introduces a figure or a policy choice that no source supplies. Ten provisions are
reproduced without amendment because they carry a recorded finding. Twenty-one further defects are recorded
and deliberately left undrafted; Addendum C lists every one with the reason nothing is proposed.

**Out of scope by decision:** the controlled-substance number requirement for certifying clinicians. It is
reproduced where the renumbering around it required it, recorded as fact, and nothing is proposed about it.

## 2. The two BLOCKING findings. Both are drafted

- **7.35.3.16 A and C(2)(b), the third-party evaluator conflict.** A program "shall engage a qualified
  third-party evaluation team", and the conflict rule disqualifies anyone "receiving compensation from... the
  program or organization being evaluated", so as published no person can serve. The cure is drafted at both
  provisions, so the committee may adopt either or both.
- **7.35.3.14 C, the registration that does not exist.** Healing center owner and employee authority is
  conditioned on being "registered with the department", and no such registration exists in 7.35.3 NMAC or
  7.35.2 NMAC. The registration is drafted at 7.35.3.11 A(11), where the healing center already files the
  names. 7.35.3.14 C itself is in the practicum draft's scope and is amended in neither document.

## 3. The defined terms. The largest finding in this scope

7.35.3.7 NMAC provides in full: "The definitions in 7.35.2.7 NMAC apply to this part." Sixteen terms that
carry regulatory consequence in Part 3 are defined in neither part. Six are drafted at 7.35.3.7 in this
document, because in each case a single provision of the published rule supplies the content: certificant,
facilitator, other approved location, practicum, registrant of another approved location, and student. Ten
are not, and the two largest are healing center, 54 occurrences, and certifying clinician, 53 occurrences.

The two definitions this draft adds beyond the four it started with are other approved location, from
Subsection B of 7.35.3.11 NMAC, which provides that "Other approved locations are physical locations at which
medical psilocybin is intended to be consumed, that are not locations of healing centers.", and practicum,
from Subsection A of 7.35.3.19 NMAC, under which an individual seeking certification "shall participate in
supervised practice training, otherwise referred to as a" practicum. The practicum provision belongs to the
practicum draft's scope; that draft amends the hour figures in the same subsection and keeps the quoted
phrase, and Addendum B records the dependency.

**Do not compose a definition** for any of the ten remaining terms, and do not report one as available. No
source in this record defines any of them, and assembling a definition from scattered provisions is a policy
act. Addendum A counts every occurrence of all sixteen, section by section, in both parts, and assembles for
each of the ten the provisions of 7.35.3 NMAC, 7.35.2 NMAC and the Medical Psilocybin Act that bear on it, so
the committee can decide from assembled material. None of the sixteen terms appears anywhere in the Medical
Psilocybin Act.

Related settled facts: "guide", the one defined term in 7.35.2.7 NMAC for an individual who assists
practitioners during administration sessions, appears zero times in 7.35.3 NMAC, while facilitator, which
7.35.2.7 NMAC does not define, appears 65 times. 7.35.2 NMAC was adopted effective June 23, 2026, so amending
7.35.2.7 NMAC is a separate rulemaking, and any term Part 3 needs has to be carried in Part 3.

## 4. The 40-hour waiver conflict. Recorded and sharpened, not resolved

For the same 40 hours of contact time, 7.35.3.10 D(1) requires "two separate group sessions" and 7.35.3.19
G(4) requires "A minimum of one group session". 7.35.3.10 D(1) is the stricter reading, and it is the
provision that names an applicant relying on an educational program from another jurisdiction. Both descend
from the July 9, 2026 draft, which carried the same waiver twice and read "or two separate group sessions" in
both places, a third reading the published rule dropped, so no restoration can conform the two provisions.
The provision is reproduced at 7.35.3.10 with a review note stating the choices, and the choice sits with the
Training and Education Committee. The practicum draft records the same conflict at its re-lettered 7.35.3.19 K
and amends neither provision. **Do not write that either provision controls**, that the conflict has been
resolved, or that either figure is a drafting error; each is a figure the published rule carries.

## 5. Hours. The reading convention, and the two hour figures in this scope

The working convention of this review: **an hour figure sits inside the governing total unless the provision
states it as an addition.** Where totals do not reconcile under that convention, the mismatch is recorded as a
question at the provision, not resolved by drafting. The program hour totals themselves belong to the
practicum draft's scope, and `amendments/SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md` is the authority for them; do not restate its
totals from this folder.

Only two hour figures appear anywhere in the twenty-four sections this document covers, and both carry a
question. The 40 hours of contact time in the waiver at 7.35.3.10 D(1), which section 4 above covers. And the
10 hours of mentoring at 7.35.3.17 A, "after graduation and after practicum hours are completed", which under
the convention sit inside no total, published or proposed: the practicum draft carries its supervision and
consultation hours inside its program totals, and no line of those totals is mentoring after graduation. If
the consultation requirement lands at the practicum draft's proposed 7.35.3.19 H while 7.35.3.17 A also
stands, a graduate owes both sets of hours; where the requirement sits is open with Dr. Metz, Ms. Wilson and
Dr. Leeman. **Do not report the 10 mentoring hours as part of any program total**, and do not report the two
obligations as reconciled.
