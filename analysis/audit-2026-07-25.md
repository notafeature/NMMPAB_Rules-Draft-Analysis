# Factual audit of docs/, July 25 2026

Scope: every page in `docs/` on `main` at commit `f04669e`, checked against the current
source documents. The rule text was extracted independently with pypdf from
`docs/documents/rules-draft-2026-07-23-published.pdf` (19 pages, 7.35.3.1 through .28);
no extraction or analysis file in the repository was used as a source. Transcripts were
extracted the same way from the PDFs in `docs/documents/`.

Report only. Nothing was changed.

Severities: **WRONG** means a reader is misled about a requirement, including a citation
that sends them to a page containing none of the cited provision. **IMPRECISE** means the
statement is defensible but loose, internally inconsistent, or off by a page inside the
cited section. **STALE** means it was true when written and is not true on July 25.

Counts: 15 WRONG, 17 IMPRECISE, 10 STALE, 7 categories of claim that could not be verified.
