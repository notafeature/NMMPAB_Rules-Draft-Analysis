# 7.35.3 NMAC, section by section delta: July 23 2026 published proposed rule to August 25 2026 revised proposed rule

The department published a revised proposed rule for 7.35.3 NMAC on August 25, 2026, together with proposed amendments to three sections of the adopted rule 7.35.2 NMAC and a notice setting the rule hearing for October 2, 2026. This file records every difference between the July 23 published text and the August 25 published text, the content of the 7.35.2 amendments against the adopted rule, and the facts fixed by the hearing notice. It was written before any page of the site was changed.

# 1. Act on this: changes to what a regulated person or program must do

**For practitioners and facilitators**

1. The practitioner and facilitator didactic module rises from 30 hours to 65 hours, and at least one third of the didactic hours must be conducted in person. [S14]
2. The simulated patient requirement rises from 5 hours to 10: five in person, and five more that may be in person or virtual, synchronous or asynchronous, each including a competency evaluation. [S15]
3. Eleven topics are added to the required didactic list, including legal considerations, cultural competencies, traditional and ceremonial practices, equity and access, informed consent, touch and somatic awareness, and continuity of care and creating care plans. Every July 23 topic survives. [S16]
4. The practicum gains an evaluation gate: the student must present at least two of their last four patients to the practicum supervisor as case presentations and receive a passing grade to complete the practicum. [S20]
5. The first 20 hours of administration day sessions must be with patients the supervising practitioner determines to be low-risk for adverse events, and same-day administration sessions must include patients with a diversity of the qualifying conditions. [S18, S19]
6. A certifying clinician, practitioner, or facilitator may not consume or be under the influence of psilocybin or any other intoxicant while providing services to a patient. [S7]
7. Certifying clinicians, practitioners, and facilitators must provide the department access to records on request. [S8]
8. Anyone who obtains, transfers, or transports medical psilocybin as a practitioner, facilitator, or healing center owner or employee must generate or verify a chain of custody form and be prepared to show identification during transport. [S9]
9. A practitioner applicant's professional license must be one to practice therapy, counseling, or behavioral services; a certifying clinician applicant's license must permit diagnosing the qualifying conditions. [S2, S3]

**For educational programs**

10. The department will review and approve or deny each proposed curriculum, initial or modified, against five stated grounds. [S10]
11. Programs must collect structured student feedback within 30 calendar days of the end of each module. [S13]
12. The third-party evaluation report gains an assessment of the program's student feedback processes. [S12]
13. Applications must list all other employees of the organization by legal name. Healing center applications must do the same. [S4, S6]
14. The department may waive, suspend, or reduce the didactic hourly and topic requirements, a new counterpart to the existing practicum waiver. [S17]

**For healing centers and other approved locations**

15. The wilderness first aid, first responder, or EMT staffing requirement and the first aid kit and AED requirement for natural-environment settings far from emergency services move from the application into an operational duty that applies during administration sessions. [S5, S25]
16. Adverse health event reports are due within two calendar days rather than two business days, and the record duties extend to registrants of other approved locations. [S24]
17. A student who has completed at least 50 practicum hours may substitute as a facilitator including for the purpose of billing. [S22]
18. The patient list duty narrows to patients who have utilized the location; the list of intended patients is dropped. [S21]

**For patients**

19. The department's decision window on a patient application becomes 30 calendar days rather than 30 business days, and the department may review medical records when verifying an application. [S1]

**Unchanged, checked provision by provision:** the practicum totals of 100 hours for facilitators and 120 for practitioners; the 80 administration-day and 20 preparation-and-integration hour minimums and the practitioner's additional 20 supervision hours; the 10 mentoring hours; the continuing education requirements of 8 and 20 hours every two years; the December 31, 2027 practicum waiver at 40 contact hours; the emergency response credential options and the two-year HIPAA certification; the test-out option; the New Mexico module; and the certifying clinician's eight-hour module. See part 5.

# 3. Full change list, 7.35.3 NMAC, in section order

Sections **.1 through .7 are word-for-word identical** between OLD and NEW, including .7, which in both texts reads in full: "DEFINITIONS: The definitions in 7.35.2.7 NMAC apply to this part." The definitions that give the rule its role vocabulary therefore live in the 7.35.2 amendments; see part 4.

OLD printed five section headings as 7.34.3 (.13, .14, .20, .23, .25). NEW corrects four of them; the .25 heading remains printed 7.34.3.25 (NEW p. 17), against its own history note and every cross-reference. EDITORIAL.

### 7.35.3.8 Patient enrollment application process (NEW pp. 1 to 2)

- **S1, SUBSTANTIVE.** The department's decision window on a completed patient application changes from 30 business days to "30 calendar days" (NEW p. 2), shortening the clock. In the verification provision, the department may act "by otherwise contacting a certifying clinician to obtain information and review medical records" (NEW p. 2); OLD ended at "information."
- EDITORIAL: the renewal filing window gains the qualifier "calendar" on its 60- and 30-day bounds; "Such additional information" becomes "Such additional information and documentation" here and in the application lists of .9 and .11; small grammar and spacing corrections.

### 7.35.3.9 Certifying clinician, practitioner, and facilitator application (NEW pp. 2 to 4)

- **S2, SUBSTANTIVE.** The certifying clinician applicant's license documentation changes from "current professional license to practice in New Mexico (e.g. MD, NP)" to "current professional license to practice in New Mexico and diagnose the qualifying conditions (e.g., MD, NP)" (NEW p. 3). A license that does not permit diagnosis no longer qualifies.
- **S3, SUBSTANTIVE.** The practitioner applicant's license documentation changes from "current professional license to practice in New Mexico (e.g. PSY, LSW, LCSW)" to "current professional license to practice therapy, counseling, or behavioral services in New Mexico" (NEW p. 3). The license type is now stated, not only exemplified.

### 7.35.3.10 Application process for certification (NEW pp. 4 to 5)

- EDITORIAL: the reciprocity waiver's "full practicum hours required by New Mexico" becomes "required by this rule"; the temporary certification validity gains the qualifier "calendar" on its 90 days. The reciprocity terms themselves are unchanged: application by December 31, 2027, and "at least 40 hours of contact time with a minimum of two separate individual sessions and two separate group sessions" (NEW p. 5).

### 7.35.3.11 Application process for healing centers and other approved locations (NEW pp. 5 to 7)

- **S4, SUBSTANTIVE.** New healing center application item: "(22) A list of all other employees of the organization, including their legal names;" (NEW p. 6).
- **S5, SUBSTANTIVE (moved, with S25).** OLD's natural-environment application items, requiring two individuals present holding wilderness first aid certification, wilderness first responder certification, or New Mexico EMT licensure when the setting is 15 minutes or more from emergency medical services, and a first aid kit and AED on site, are struck from both the healing center and the other approved location application lists. The duty reappears as an operational requirement at .20 K; see S25. What was checked at application is now enforced during administration sessions.
- EDITORIAL: the duplicative item "Any additional documentation reasonably requested by the department" is struck from the application list (the closing catch-all remains) and the items renumber; the renewal window gains "calendar."

### 7.35.3.12 Application process for psilocybin educational programs (NEW pp. 7 to 8)

- **S6, SUBSTANTIVE.** New application item: "(23) A list of all other employees of the organization, including their legal names;" (NEW p. 7).
- EDITORIAL: the duplicative additional-documentation item is struck and the list renumbers; "résumés" becomes "resumes."

### 7.35.3.13 Requirements and prohibitions (NEW pp. 8 to 9)

- **S7, SUBSTANTIVE.** New subsection:

  > "F. Certifying clinicians, practitioners, and facilitators; prohibition against intoxicants: A certifying clinician, practitioner, or facilitator shall not consume or otherwise be under the influence of psilocybin or any other intoxicant when providing services to a patient." (NEW p. 9)

- **S8, SUBSTANTIVE.** New subsection:

  > "G. Record access: Certifying clinicians, practitioners, and facilitators shall timely provide the department access to records upon the department's request." (NEW p. 9)

- EDITORIAL: "formal consult" becomes "formal consultation" in the telemedicine provision here and in .8; "initial integrative session" becomes plural in the data submission list.

### 7.35.3.14 Authorized possession, purchase, or sale; chain of custody (NEW p. 9)

- **S9, SUBSTANTIVE.** The heading gains "; CHAIN OF CUSTODY" and a new paragraph (D) requires a practitioner, facilitator, or healing center owner or employee who obtains or transfers medical psilocybin to generate and submit a chain of custody form through the department's electronic system, or to verify a producer-generated form on receipt, and to "be prepared to show proper identification, including an employee badge, driver's license, vehicle registration and proof of insurance, and the appropriate chain of custody form to law enforcement and the department when requested" while transporting (NEW p. 9). This pairs with the AMEND changes to 7.35.2.24; see part 4.

### 7.35.3.15 Educational programs; required reporting and curriculum approval (NEW pp. 9 to 10)

- **S10, SUBSTANTIVE.** A new subsection A puts curriculum approval in the department's hands for initial applications and subsequent modifications, with five grounds for denial: failure to meet the overall minimum hour requirements; failure to meet the specific requirements for each module; materials presenting "incorrect or outdated information, or information that poses a substantial threat to public health or safety"; a curriculum that "does not clearly outline how the required topics will be covered in sufficient depth for students to understand them"; or a determination that it "otherwise ... fails to meet the educational objectives of this rule" (NEW pp. 9 to 10). OLD's subsections reletter.
- **S11, SUBSTANTIVE.** Required update reporting extends from instructional staff changes to "Any change in instructional or other staff" (NEW p. 10).

### 7.35.3.16 Requirements for third-party evaluators (NEW pp. 10 to 11)

- **S12, SUBSTANTIVE.** The evaluation report gains a new required element, "(5) An assessment of student feedback processes and a summary of the feedback" (NEW p. 11), and the later items renumber to (8).

### 7.35.3.17 Educational programs; mentoring requirements; record-keeping (NEW p. 11)

- **S13, SUBSTANTIVE.** New subsection:

  > "F. Educational programs shall collect feedback from students within 30 calendar days of the completion of each module, which shall include, at a minimum, ratings and qualitative responses regarding the quality, quantity, and satisfaction of the students with the course pedagogy, materials, presentations, lectures, assignments, evaluations, and course content." (NEW p. 11)

- The 10 mentoring hours, their 12-month validity, the no-additional-charge condition, the test-out option and its one-fourth price cap, and the five-year record duties are unchanged.

### 7.35.3.18 Educational requirements (NEW pp. 11 to 13)

The section that changed most. The New Mexico module (A) and the certifying clinician's eight-hour module (B) are unchanged.

- **S14, SUBSTANTIVE.** The practitioner and facilitator therapy module rises from a minimum of 30 didactic hours to 65, with a new in-person floor:

  > "C. Requirements for initial practitioner and facilitator certification: All practitioners and facilitators shall complete a psilocybin therapy module consisting of a minimum of 65 didactic hours with at least one third of the didactic hours conducted in person, and 10 hours of simulated patient experience, prior to applying for certification." (NEW p. 12)

  OLD read "a minimum of 30 didactic hours and 5 hours of simulated patient experience." With the unchanged five-hour role-specific modules (D for facilitators, E for practitioners), total pre-certification module time for a practitioner or facilitator moves from 40 hours to 80: 70 didactic plus 10 simulated patient. The New Mexico module carries no stated hours in either text.

- **S15, SUBSTANTIVE.** The simulated patient requirement doubles and splits:

  > "(2) An in-person simulated patient experience of no less than five hours,; and an additional simulated patient experience of no less than five hours, which may be in- person, virtual, either synchronous or asynchronous, and which shall include evaluation of the student to demonstrate competency. These shall include evaluation of the student to demonstrate competency." (NEW pp. 12 to 13; the stray ",;" and the doubled final sentence are the rule's, see part 6)

- **S16, SUBSTANTIVE.** The didactic topic list restructures from numbered items (1) through (17) to lettered items (a) through (aa) under a new lead-in, "The didactic portion, which shall address the following topics at a minimum" (NEW p. 12). Every OLD topic survives. Eleven topics are added: safety, risk mitigation, and harm reduction practices; drug interactions and pharmacology as its own topic; legal considerations; cultural competencies; traditional and ceremonial practices and considerations; equity and access; education on the qualifying conditions and appropriate treatment practices; evidence-informed practices and research updates; informed consent; touch and somatic awareness; and continuity of care and creating care plans. The emergencies topic expands to "Psychedelic emergencies, urgencies, complications, and medical monitoring." No topic carries an hour minimum.

- **S17, SUBSTANTIVE.** A new waiver subsection mirrors the practicum waiver:

  > "H. Waiver of didactic requirements: The department may waive, temporarily suspend, or otherwise reduce the didactic hourly and topic requirements for individuals applying for certification in order to facilitate the certification of individuals trained by other programs, to build the initial infrastructure of the program, or if subsequent evaluations of educational programs determine the changes are appropriate for ensuring competency of the applicants for certification." (NEW p. 13)

- Unchanged: the emergency response credential options (BLS, or CPR and AED, or New Mexico EMT licensure) and the two-year HIPAA certification (F); continuing education at eight hours for certifying clinicians and 20 for practitioners and facilitators every two years (G).

### 7.35.3.19 Practicum requirements (NEW pp. 13 to 14)

The totals are unchanged: "a minimum of 100 hours of supervised practice training for facilitators and 120 hours for practitioners" (NEW p. 13), built from 80 administration-day hours, 20 preparation-and-integration hours, and the practitioner's additional 20 supervision hours. The patient counts (14 different patients over at least eight sessions, six individual, two group) are unchanged.

- **S18, SUBSTANTIVE.** New administration-day criterion: "(3) The initial 20 hours of administration day sessions shall be with patients who are determined by the supervising practitioner to be low-risk for adverse events or other complications." (NEW p. 14)
- **S19, SUBSTANTIVE.** New administration-day criterion: "(4) Same-day administration sessions shall include patients who have a diversity of the qualifying conditions." (NEW p. 14)
- **S20, SUBSTANTIVE.** A new evaluation gate inside the existing hours:

  > "C. Practicum evaluation: As part of the minimum required practicum hours, the student shall present a minimum of two of the last four patients to the practicum supervisor as case presentations for evaluation, and must receive a passing grade on the case presentations to complete their practicum." (NEW p. 14)

  OLD's subsections C through G reletter to D through H.
- EDITORIAL: the B heading corrects from "in-person and integration sessions" to "preparation and integration sessions." The December 31, 2027 waiver (now H) is unchanged: 40 hours of contact time, two individual sessions and one group session, each spanning preparation, administration, and integration.

### 7.35.3.20 Requirements for healing centers and other approved locations (NEW pp. 14 to 16)

- **S21, SUBSTANTIVE.** The patient list duty narrows from "all qualified patients who are intended to utilize, and who have utilized, the location" to "all qualified patients who have utilized the location for administration sessions" (NEW p. 14).
- **S22, SUBSTANTIVE.** The student substitution provision now reaches billing: a student is "deemed qualified to substitute as a facilitator, including for the purpose of billing," once registered with a certified educational program and past 50 practicum hours (NEW p. 15).
- **S23, clarifying.** The department may waive or decrease "this requirement" where OLD said "this ratio" in the staffing ratio exception (NEW p. 15).
- **S24, SUBSTANTIVE.** Adverse health event reports are due "no later than two calendar days" after awareness, where OLD said business days, and the adverse-event record duty runs to "Healing centers and registrants of other approved locations" (NEW p. 15).
- **S25, SUBSTANTIVE (moved, with S5).** The natural-environment requirement returns as an operational duty:

  > "K. A healing center, and a registrant of another approved location, that conducts administration sessions in a natural environment setting that is 15 minutes or more away from emergency medical service response or a hospital, urgent care, or other emergency medical services shall ensure that there is a basic first aid kit and an AED on site, and that there are at least two individuals present during administration sessions who are not receiving treatment and who have: (1) Wilderness first aid certification; (2) Wilderness first responder certification; or (3) Licensure as a New Mexico emergency medical technician (e.g., EMT-basic, EMT- intermediate, EMT-paramedic)." (NEW p. 15)

  OLD's K and L reletter to L and M.
- **S26, SUBSTANTIVE.** New subsection: "N. A healing center and a registrant of another approved location shall timely provide the department access to records, upon the department's request." (NEW p. 16)

### 7.35.3.21 through 7.35.3.28 (NEW pp. 16 to 20)

EDITORIAL only: the .23 heading misnumber corrects; .24 tidies "through the electronic system designated by the department"; punctuation corrections in .25 and .27. The administrative review committee that .25 relies on is newly defined in the 7.35.2.7 amendments; the review process itself is unchanged.

# 4. The 7.35.2 amendments against the adopted rule

The amendments document opens: "This is an amendment to 7.35.2 NMAC, Sections 7, 10, and 22, effective xx/xx, 2026" (AMEND p. 1). Its body amends sections 7, 10, and 24; on the header's "22," see part 6.

### 7.35.2.7 Definitions (AMEND pp. 1 to 4)

7.35.3 defines nothing of its own, so these definitions are the rule's vocabulary. Twenty-one defined terms are added: administrative review committee (the chief medical officer, a deputy secretary, and the chief nursing officer, or designees), adverse health event, certificant, certifying clinician, educational program, electronic system, enrollment, facilitator, healing center, integration session, medical psilocybin service, New Mexico module, other approved location, practicum, preparation session, producer, registrant of another approved location, sachet (correcting the adopted text's "Satchet"), secretary, sex offender, and sex offense.

The definitions that settle the role names:

> "'Certifying clinician' means a clinician who holds a state of New Mexico controlled substances number, who is certified by the department to diagnose or confirm a previous diagnosis of a qualifying condition and to evaluate the medical appropriateness of a patient enrolling in the New Mexico psilocybin program." (AMEND p. 1)

> "'Practitioner' means [an individual who is a licensed healthcare professional] a clinician who is certified by the department to provide medical psilocybin integrative therapy, supervise [guides] facilitators, and who has completed department required trainings." (AMEND p. 3; bracketed text is struck)

> "'Facilitator' or 'guide' means an individual who has completed training and education approved by the department to be able to work with patients and practitioners during medical psilocybin services and who has been certified by the department." (AMEND p. 2)

The adopted rule's standalone "Guide" definition is struck; guide becomes an alias of facilitator. The published texts use the names certifying clinician, practitioner, and facilitator throughout; the terms "medical screener" and "licensed provider" appear nowhere in any of the three August 25 documents. The controlled substances number requirement for certifying clinicians now sits inside the definition itself.

Also revised: "Certification" now runs to a clinician, a practitioner, a facilitator, a healing center or other approved location, or an educational program "to participate in the medical psilocybin program," where the adopted text said "to provide medical psilocybin services to qualified patients"; and "Administration session" becomes the session in which medical psilocybin is "consumed," not "administered," by a qualified patient (AMEND p. 1).

### 7.35.2.10 General producer requirements (AMEND pp. 4 to 5)

The adopted section's lettered subsections A through H become numbered items (1) through (8) under a new lead-in. The substantive change is in the sale provision, adopted subsection G, now item (7): producers may sell psilocybin "to other producers, healing centers, and practitioners." The adopted text permitted sale only "to other producers and to practitioners." Healing centers become authorized buyers, which the healing center definition and 7.35.3.14 assume.

### 7.35.2.24 Transportation of psilocybin (AMEND pp. 5 to 6)

Transportation opens beyond producer permittees. Transport may be conducted by "persons holding a permit or designated employees, or contractors of a permittee, certified practitioner, or certified healing center" (AMEND p. 5; the adopted text ended at "permittee"). The verification duty on receipt moves from "the permittee" to "the person receiving the psilocybin shipment," and the rejection prohibition from "Permittees" to "Persons" (AMEND p. 5). Together with 7.35.3.14 (D), this builds the chain of custody for the treatment side of the program.

# 6. Defects and inconsistencies in the published documents

Recorded as facts about the documents, not corrected anywhere on the site.

1. The amendments document's header names "Sections 7, 10, and 22"; its body amends sections 7, 10, and 24, and the notice names 7.35.2.7, 7.35.2.10(G), and 7.35.2.24. No amendment to 7.35.2.22 appears in the document (AMEND p. 1).
2. The notice cites "7.35.2.10(G)"; the amendment restructures the section so that the adopted subsection G becomes item (7) of subsection A (NOTICE p. 1; AMEND p. 4).
3. 7.35.3.18 (C)(2) carries a stray ",;" after "five hours" and states its evaluation sentence twice (NEW pp. 12 to 13; quoted in full at S15).
4. The administration-day criteria list in 7.35.3.19 (A) places "; and" after item (2) although items (3) and (4) follow, and item (3) ends in a period rather than a semicolon (NEW p. 14).
5. Persisting from July 23, unchanged: certification by reciprocity requires two individual and two group sessions (7.35.3.10, p. 5), while the practicum-hours waiver for the same December 31, 2027 window requires two individual sessions and one group session (7.35.3.19 (H), p. 14).
