## Conventions used in this file

1. All quotes are verbatim from the extracted PDF text, reproduced inside fenced blocks. Hard line breaks inside quotes are wraps introduced by the PDF, not by the rule. Irregular internal spacing (for example `9 -7-6`, `activi ties`, `certif ication`) is present in the extracted text and is left alone. Where a quoted passage crosses a PDF page boundary, the page marker and the blank lines around it are omitted and nothing else is; such quotes carry both page numbers in the citation line above them.
2. "SUBSTANTIVE" means the change alters an obligation, a right, a deadline, or an eligibility condition of any party, including the department. The short action list in the next section is the narrower subset that changes what a facilitator, practitioner, certifying clinician, or educational program must actually do.
3. "STRUCTURAL" means content moved, was renumbered, or was recombined with its operative meaning materially intact.
4. "EDITORIAL" means wording, capitalization, numeral style, typo, or grammar with no change in obligation.
5. Substantive changes are numbered S1 through S90 and the numbering runs in July 23 section order.
6. Where a difference may be an artifact of PDF text extraction rather than a real difference in the rule, it is labeled "possible extraction artifact" and not counted as a change.

## Correction to one premise supplied with this assignment

The premise that didactic hours are unchanged is **not correct** for practitioners and facilitators. The July 9 draft set that module at **25** didactic hours with the 5 simulated patient hours stated as being "in addition to the 25 didactic hours". The July 23 published rule sets it at **30** didactic hours "and 5 hours of simulated patient experience". Total pre-certification module time for practitioners and facilitators therefore moves from 30 hours to 35 hours. This is change S48 below, and it is the single most consequential training-hours change in the published rule.

Every other training number named in the premise did survive unchanged, and is documented with quotes in the section "Unchanged despite the July 17 deferral" at the end of this file.

# 1. Act on this: changes to what a regulated person or program must do

These are the changes with direct operational effect. The bracketed identifier points to the full quoted entry in part 3.

**For practitioners and facilitators**

1. The practitioner and facilitator didactic module rises from 25 hours to 30 hours, with 5 simulated patient hours stated alongside it rather than folded into it. [S48]
2. Three new required curriculum topics: Trauma-Informed Care, Dosing, and Non-ordinary states of consciousness. [S49]
3. The practicum may not begin until the student has completed at least half of the didactic requirements and all of the simulated patient requirements. [S54]
4. New Mexico emergency medical technician licensure is now accepted in place of BLS or CPR plus AED, at application, in the training module, and for continuing education currency. [S10, S51, S53]
5. HIPAA certification must now have been completed within the preceding two years, rather than merely being current. [S9, S52]
6. Facilitators must now submit a completed W-9 form. [S11]
7. A facilitator's scope of work is now defined and limited: peer support plus logistical and administrative support, under the direct supervision of a practitioner, with no patient care outside that scope unless another license permits it. [S39]
8. Practitioners and facilitators must, on request, make reasonable efforts to provide the psilocybin product information document translated and in an accessible format. [S40]
9. Practitioners and facilitators must forward a producer's failed-lot test notification to the patient who consumed the product. [S41]
10. The practicum waiver deadline moves from June 30, 2027 to December 31, 2027, and the qualifying evidence changes: proof must be by logs or other records, and the applicant must show two individual sessions **and** one group session, each covering preparation, administration, and integration, rather than two individual **or** two group sessions. [S55, S59, S60]

**For applicants relying on an educational program from another jurisdiction**

11. The waiver deadline moves from December 31, 2026 to December 31, 2027, and the session condition changes from "or" to "and": two separate individual sessions **and** two separate group sessions. [S14, S15]
12. The express requirement that a reciprocal practitioner applicant complete the 20 supervisory practicum hours in New Mexico is deleted, as is the partial-hours transfer mechanism. [S16]
13. Applications received on or after January 1, 2028 must include a third-party evaluation of the out-of-jurisdiction curriculum demonstrating equivalency. [S19]
14. Oregon and Colorado program recognition is now qualified by "as of December 31, 2027". [S20]
15. The requirement to document active practitioner or facilitator licensure or certification in the other jurisdiction is deleted. [S22]

**For certifying clinicians**

16. The pre-certification in-person evaluation changes from a "physical or mental evaluation" to a "physical evaluation and medical clearance". [S37]
17. Telemedicine certification is expanded from one pathway to three, including record review and formal consult routes. [S3, S38]
18. Certifying clinicians must now document HIPAA certification completed within the preceding two years. [S9]

**For educational programs**

19. The two-faculty-member deadline moves from June 30, 2027 to December 31, 2027. [S32]
20. A program applying on or before December 31, 2027 may be certified without the third-party evaluation report, but must submit it by December 31, 2027 or the certification is rescinded. Applications after that date must include it. [S33]
21. Applications must include proof of compliance with disability accessibility laws. [S31]
22. Enrollment records must now include transcripts as well as examination results. [S47]

**For healing centers and other approved locations**

23. Concrete staffing ratios replace a reference to a ratio "required by the department": one practitioner and one facilitator per individual session; in group sessions, one practitioner per eight patients and one facilitator or qualified student per two patients, with a qualified student defined as registered with a certified educational program and having completed at least 50 practicum hours. [S65]
24. The required roster changes from certified individuals using the location to qualified patients intended to use and who have used the location. [S62]
25. Students completing their practicums may now be present during an administration session without separate patient consent. [S63]
26. A natural environment setting 15 minutes or more from emergency medical services must now also have a basic first aid kit and an AED on site. [S24]
27. Healing center certification is valid two years; other approved location certification is valid 90 days. [S28]

## Structural moves worth naming

**July 9 content split across two or more July 23 sections**

- July 9 "PSILOCYBIN EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS; REQUIRED REPORTING AND CURRICULUM APPROVAL; RELINQUISHMENT OF CERTIFICATE" (OLD p2) split three ways: A and B stay in 7.35.3.15; D, the renewal window, moves to 7.35.3.12 C; E, voluntary relinquishment, moves to 7.35.3.26. C, the enumerated denial grounds, has no successor.
- July 9 "Patient Application and Record:" (OLD p10) split three ways: the patient and clinician submittals go to 7.35.3.8; the clinician-patient relationship and telemedicine text goes to 7.34.3.13 A; the prohibition-from-certifying grounds go to 7.35.3.27 B.
- July 9 "Certifying Clinician, Practitioner, and Facilitator prohibition:" (OLD p7) split two ways: the consanguinity bullet goes to 7.34.3.13 E; the ownership bullet becomes its own section, 7.34.3.23.
- July 9 "PRACTITIONER AND FACILITATOR PRACTICUM REQUIREMENTS" subsection E (OLD p5) is the source for 7.35.3.19 E, while the same section's waiver provisions split between 7.35.3.19 F and G.

**July 9 content merged into a single July 23 section**

- The standalone July 9 reciprocity section (OLD p6 to p7) and the unnumbered "Reciprocal Practitioner or Facilitator:" application bullets (OLD p9 to p10) are folded together into 7.35.3.10. This confirms the premise supplied with this assignment.
- The July 9 healing center application section (OLD p11) and the "Other Approved Location Requirements" section (OLD p12) merge into 7.35.3.11 as subsections A and B.
- Four unnumbered July 9 blocks (OLD p13 and p14) merge into 7.34.3.20.
- The unnumbered "Testing out of the didactic portion of the required curriculum" block (OLD p7) merges into 7.35.3.17 as new subsection B.
- The July 9 educational program application section (OLD p1) merges with the renewal and denial machinery to form 7.35.3.12.

**Reordering**

The July 9 draft opened with educational program provisions and placed patient and applicant material in the middle. The July 23 rule reorders to: general provisions, then application processes in the order patient, individual certificant, out-of-jurisdiction certificant, location, educational program; then substantive requirements; then oversight, prohibitions, complaints, review, and appeals.
