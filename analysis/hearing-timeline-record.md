## Why this record exists

The board voted on July 17 to send the practicum and didactic hours back to the Training and Education Committee. The rule the department published on July 23 carries those hours unchanged. This file establishes exactly what was and was not said, so that anyone making that point can make it accurately.

## 1. The board defers the hours, 7-0

Morning session. The chair puts the question:

> "We will now go ahead and if there is not objection, we can move to a vote on to defer the training and education practicum and didactic hours to the training and education committee for them to solve to reassess and re-come together and have time to come up with those updated standards. Are there any objections to holding the vote?"

And records the result:

> "So we'll go ahead and move to defer those to the Training and Education Committee. If you are in favor of moving those practicum and didactic hours back to the Training and Education Committee, please raise your hand at this time. Cool. I see seven hands. Perfect, you can lower your hands at this time. While I know there is not Casper the ghost in the room with us, I do have to give option. If anyone would object to this, please raise your hand at this time. No, Casper, the motion is adopted. We will send that back to the Training and Education Committee."

Speaker: Ian Dunn, board chair. Basis: he called the meeting to order by name and is presiding throughout this passage.

Seven board members were present. The vote was unanimous with no objection.

## 2. The department announces the hearing window, same morning

Later in the same meeting:

> "I do want to let everybody know that because of some of the timing issues, in order from, you know, to be able to meet the goal of having the first patients in December, we are going to move forward with a regulation hearing. And we are looking at the end of August. Now, of course, we still have some of the training. And so some of those aspects that are being developed and considered. And so we still do have those portions."

Speaker: Dominic Zurlo, director, New Mexico Department of Health Center for Medical Cannabis and Psilocybin. Basis: the speaker says "we are moving forward from the department standpoint," and in the afternoon session the same speaker says "as I mentioned this morning during the board meeting" while identifying as the department.

Read this passage carefully. Two things are said together: the hearing is set for the end of August, and the training aspects are "being developed and considered," with "those portions" still outstanding.

## 3. The committee is told to hurry, with no deadline

Speaker: Dominic Zurlo. Basis: the committee chair addresses the question to "Dominic" and thanks "Dominic" immediately after the answer.

No date was attached to that request. The committee was not given a deadline to deliver.

## 6. What the department published on July 23

The proposed rule was published July 23, 2026, six days after the deferral vote. On the two numbers the board sent back:

Practicum, 7.35.3.19 (A), published text:

> "The practicum shall consist of a minimum of 100 hours of supervised practice training for facilitators and 120 hours for practitioners and shall be completed prior to applying for certification."

Practitioner supervision, 7.35.3.19 (C), published text:

> "Practitioners shall complete an additional minimum of 20 hours as a practitioner supervising facilitators during in-person administration day sessions"

Didactic, 7.35.3.18 (C), published text:

> "All practitioners and facilitators shall complete a psilocybin therapy module consisting of a minimum of 30 didactic hours and 5 hours of simulated patient experience, prior to applying for certification"

These are the July 9 numbers, carried forward unchanged. See `july23-published-delta.md` for the full comparison.

## 8. What this record does and does not support

Stating this accurately matters more than stating it forcefully. The transcripts support some claims and undercut others.

**Supported by the record:**

- The board voted unanimously to send the practicum and didactic hours back to committee, and the rule published six days later carries those hours unchanged.
- On the morning of the vote the department described the training aspects as "being developed and considered," with "those portions" still outstanding, in the same breath as announcing the hearing.
- The committee was asked for input "sooner rather than later" and was given no deadline.
- The committee's next meeting, August 21, falls after publication and seven days before the hearing. Whatever the committee produces on August 21 arrives after the proposed text is fixed and inside the comment window, not before it.

**Not supported by the record:**

- That the hearing timing was withheld. It was not. "The end of August" was stated in the morning board meeting and restated in the afternoon committee meeting as "about six weeks from now," which lands on August 28. Anyone at either meeting was told the window.

The narrower and stronger version of the concern: the window was communicated, the publication was not. Nothing in either transcript tells the committee that the proposed rule would be published within a week, or that it would carry forward the two numbers the board had just voted to send back for reassessment. The committee was asked for hours "sooner rather than later" on the same day the text containing those hours was six days from publication.

That is the claim the record will carry. The other one it will not.
