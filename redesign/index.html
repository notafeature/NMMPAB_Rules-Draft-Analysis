Practicum and didactic hours

On July 17 the Advisory Board voted 7-0 to send the practicum and didactic hours to the Training and Education Committee. The rule published on July 23 carries the department's own figures unchanged: 100 practicum hours for facilitators, 120 for practitioners, with the shared didactic module raised from 25 hours to 30.

The clock: the committee's recommendation has not yet reached the department. The committee next meets August 21; the hearing is August 28. The department has said input is wanted sooner rather than later.

What changed on July 23

The three changes that matter in the published text, against the July 9 draft.

  • The shared didactic module went from 25 hours to 30, 7.35.3.18 (C), while the hours were deferred. Three topics were added: trauma-informed care, dosing, and non-ordinary states of consciousness.
  • The practicum figures are unchanged: 100 and 120 hours, 7.35.3.19 (A).
  • New gate: the practicum cannot begin until half the didactic and all simulated-patient requirements are complete, 7.35.3.19 (A). No counterpart in the July 9 draft; not raised at either July 17 meeting.
