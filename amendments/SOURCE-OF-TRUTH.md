## 1. Students and the medicine. This question is closed

A site page must not say, suggest, or imply that a student lacks authority to take part in a practicum session, or
that the rule has a gap about student possession of psilocybin. It does not. Four provisions and one statement on the
record settle it.

| Where | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| 7.35.3.14, page 9 | Purchase, possession, sale and administration belong to the practitioner, the facilitator, and healing center owners and employees. Subsection A: a practitioner "may sell or otherwise provide medical psilocybin products to qualified patients". Subsection C: a healing center may "sell or otherwise administer those products to qualified patients" |
| 7.35.3.19 A, page 13 | A student may "experience, observe, or conduct supervised facilitation of administration day sessions". Facilitation is the supervised work; it is not the act of providing or administering the medicine |
| 7.35.3.19 B, page 13 | A student may "experience, observe, or conduct (when licensure allows) preparation or integration sessions". The rule itself limits a student's conduct by licensure rather than by a possession rule |
| 7.35.3.20 D, page 14 | "students completing their practicums may be present during an administration session" |
| 7.35.3.20 H(5), page 14 | A qualified student counts toward the group staffing ratio at one per two patients. A student is qualified when "registered with a certified educational program" and having "completed at least 50 hours of their practicum" |
| 7.35.3.17 C(2)(e), page 11 | A program must record the "Names of facilitators supervised by practitioner students during practicum", so the rule already contemplates a practitioner student supervising facilitators |
| July 17, 2026 Training and Education Committee transcript | The department stated a training permit "really wouldn't be necessary with the model that we have" because a student is covered "as them being a registered student with an educational program where they have that ability to be a part of those training sessions, including to conduct them under supervision", and that "those students already will have that kind of protection ability to be there as a part of being registered as a student with an educational program" |

**The correct statement for a site page:** the medicine is provided once, at the start of an administration session, by
the practitioner or the healing center. A facilitator, and a student acting in a facilitator role under supervision,
does the facilitation work. No training permit is proposed anywhere in the draft, and none is needed. The phrase
"training permit" appears nowhere in the rule as published.

**Do not write:** that a student needs a permit; that the practicum is unauthorized; that there is a possession gap;
that a training permit was recommended and rejected. The department's position is that registered-student status
already carries it, and 7.35.3.20 H(5) as published is the staffing consequence.

## 2. Hours. These figures are final in the draft and arithmetic-checked

| Component | Published | Draft v8 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Mexico module | not stated | 6 | +6 |
| Psilocybin therapy module, didactic | 30 | 68 | +38 |
| Simulated patient experience | 5 | 5 | 0 |
| Role-specific module | 5 | 5 | 0 |
| **Module total, either permit** | **40** | **84** | **+44** |
| Practicum, facilitator | 100 | 62 | -38 |
| Practicum, licensed provider | 120 | 72 | -48 |
| Supervision or consultation | 10 | 20 | +10 |
| **Program total, facilitator** | **150** | **166** | **+16** |
| **Program total, licensed provider** | **170** | **176** | **+6** |

Practicum steps: 30 + 20 + 12 = 62 for the facilitator; plus 10 supervisory hours = 72 for the licensed provider.
Source: Metz recommendation pages 3 and 4. Every step figure is that document's own, stated as "approximately".

**Total hours rise. They do not fall.** A page that says the recommendation reduces training is wrong. Practicum hours
fall; module hours rise by more.

**One published figure is ambiguous and the site must not pick a side.** 7.35.3.19 C adds "an additional minimum of
20 hours" without saying what it is additional to. Inside the 120 the published licensed provider total is 170; on top
of the 120 it is 190. The draft follows the recommendation and treats supervision as inside. Say both readings exist.

## 3. The one thing that needs the Legislature, and the version that does not

The first practicum stage is drafted in two versions at Paragraph (1) of Subsection D of 7.35.3.19. A site page must
present both, not one.

**Version 1 works under the Medical Psilocybin Act exactly as it stands today.** The initial experience is performed
with qualified patients, in an observing or assisting role under supervision. No new section is needed and proposed
7.35.3.29 is deleted. The practicum total is still 62 and 72 hours.

**Version 2 is the model the Metz recommendation describes and needs a statutory amendment.** The initial experience
is performed with well participants in a retreat or peer-support model. Paragraph (2) of Subsection B of Section 5 of
the Act makes lawful only "a clinician administering or a qualified patient taking psilocybin in an approved setting",
and Subsection H of Section 3 defines a qualified patient by diagnosis with a qualifying condition, so a well
participant falls outside it. Subsection D of Section 5 protects a person "for simply being in the presence of the
medical use of psilocybin", which reaches presence and not taking. Proposed 7.35.3.29 states the amendment requested
in its own Subsection D, and Subsection C conditions the whole section on that amendment, so the section cannot
operate before the law changes.

## 4. Citation form. Two rules the site currently gets wrong

**Cite the statute, never the bill.** Write "the Medical Psilocybin Act, Section 5(B)(2)". Do not write "SB 219",
"Senate Bill 219", or "SB0219" in body text. Section 1 of the Act provides that "Sections 1 through 11 of this act may
be cited as the" Medical Psilocybin Act, so the Act's own internal section numbers are the correct citation. **Do not
assert an NMSA 1978 section number for the Act anywhere.** The codification has not been verified in this repository,
and earlier drafts asserted 26-2D-1 through -11 without a source. The bill PDF may be listed as the text consulted.

**The permit renaming is a rename, not a scope change.** Recommendation 1 of the Metz recommendation renames the
practitioner permit to licensed provider. Write "renaming" or "the new permit name". It reaches no hour count and no
practicum requirement. It touches 66 occurrences across fourteen of the twenty-eight sections of 7.35.3 NMAC, of which
the draft conforms 29, and 12 more in 7.35.2 NMAC, which is already adopted and would be a separate rulemaking.

## 6. Defects recorded in the draft but not fixed, because the fix sits outside its scope

- **7.35.3.14 C.** Healing center staff authority is conditioned on being "registered with the department", and no such
  registration exists in 7.35.3 NMAC or 7.35.2 NMAC. The fix belongs at 7.35.3.11 or 7.35.3.20.
- **7.35.3.18 A.** Every certification pathway is conditioned on a New Mexico module the department has not committed
  to a date for. No source supplies a date.
- **7.35.3.19 K against 7.35.3.10 D(1).** For the same 40 hours of contact time, one requires "A minimum of one group
  session" and the other requires "two separate group sessions".
- **Section headings.** Five read 7.34.3 instead of 7.35.3. The draft corrects two, 7.34.3.14 and 7.34.3.20. Three
  remain: 7.34.3.13, 7.34.3.23, 7.34.3.25.
- **7.35.3.18 F.** The sentence as published does not complete. The draft repairs it without changing any obligation.

## 7. Terms used in Part 3 and defined nowhere

7.35.3.7 provides in full: "The definitions in 7.35.2.7 NMAC apply to this part." These appear in 7.35.2 NMAC zero
times: **facilitator**, **healing center**, **certifying clinician**, **student**. The nearest analogue to facilitator
is "guide", an individual who assists practitioners during administration sessions and is registered with the
department, which carries no professional license. A site page may state this as a finding. It is not addressed by the
draft.
