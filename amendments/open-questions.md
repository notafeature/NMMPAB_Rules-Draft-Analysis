## Open, needs a decision from someone

**Practicum supervisors, 7.35.3.19 F.** The precedent cited on the record is an analogy, not a rule: the July 17 committee transcript records "the apprenticeship or the site supervisor model, similar to what they do for counseling licensure or direct entry midwifery." New Mexico's rules for either have not been checked and are not in this repository. Nothing else in Part 3 registers or qualifies a supervisor of any kind.

It is drafted because 7.35.3.19 E confines all practicum hours to approved locations, which is the placement half of blocking defect B4, and because a student whose program has no co-located site otherwise has no route. Three options:

1. Keep both. 7.35.3.19 E opens placement, F registers and publishes supervisors.
2. Keep E, cut F to one sentence requiring the supervisor to hold the applicable certification, and leave vetting to the department. Removes the invented one-year floor and the published list.
3. Cut both. The placement bottleneck is then unaddressed and the practicum stays confined to a student's own program's sites.

Option 2 is the recommendation if the invented machinery is the objection.

## For counsel

**Facilitator is not a defined term.** 7.35.3.7 imports the definitions in 7.35.2.7 NMAC. "Facilitator" appears zero times in 7.35.2 NMAC. Neither do "healing center", "certifying clinician", or "student". 7.35.2.7 does define "guide" as a person who assists {{PTS}} during administration sessions and is registered with the department, and defines a {{PT}} as one who supervises guides, which describes the same role Part 3 calls a facilitator.

Two consequences: every facilitator provision in Part 3 rests on an undefined term, and a facilitator, like a guide, holds no professional license and so is not a "clinician" under Section 3(B) of the Act. 7.35.3.14 (B) authorizes facilitators to possess and provide psilocybin. Section 5 of the Act names producers, clinicians and qualified patients, and protects everyone else for presence only.

A {{PT}} is not affected. 7.35.2.7's definition requires a licensed healthcare professional, which matches the Act's "clinician".

See `blocking-defects.md`, "The statutory frame", item 3.
