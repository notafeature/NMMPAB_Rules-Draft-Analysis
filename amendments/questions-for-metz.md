# Open questions for Dr. Metz

Short list. Reasoning for each is in `metz-crosswalk.md` and `blocking-defects.md`.

## Answered, drafted accordingly

1. **Is the 84 inclusive of the 5 simulated-patient hours?** Inclusive. Drafted as 84 module hours with a didactic floor of 79 inside it.
2. **Are the roughly 30 well-participant hours inside the 62 and 72, or additional?** Inside. And if well participants are unavailable, the practicum bucket holds its size with the carve-out made later, which is what the two limbs of 7.35.3.19 D(1) do.
3. **Ethics at roughly 20 percent: of what?** Resolved internally as 20 percent of the 70-hour core module, so 14 hours. Confirm rather than decide.

## Settled by the statute, not by her

4. **Step 1 with well participants.** Section 5(B)(2) of the Medical Psilocybin Act makes lawful "a clinician administering or a qualified patient taking psilocybin in an approved setting." A well participant is not a qualified patient under Section 3(H). A statutory change is required. 7.35.3.29 keeps its contingency clause and becomes the ask rather than the assertion. See `blocking-defects.md`, "The statutory frame."

## Open

5. **Practicum at 90 rather than 72 for a practitioner.** With consultation drafted at 20, only 90 clears the published requirement on the reading that puts it at 190. At 72 the total is 176, fourteen short. Content to take 90?
6. **Consultation at 20.** Drafted at the bottom of the range, since a range in a rule enacts its floor. The argument for 30 is that practicum step 2 is limited to low-acuity cases, so consultation is the only place a trainee meets complexity under supervision before full permitting. Keep 20, or go to 30?
7. **Acuity during consultation.** No document says anything about it and the draft does not either. Should the rule?
8. **May a practicum participant be another student?** 7.35.3.29 B does not exclude it.
9. **Supervisory hours inside the practicum total.** Drafted as inside, since 62 plus 10 equals 72. Confirm.
10. **Site supervisor experience floor.** Drafted at one year. No figure appears in any source. One year, higher, or competency-based?
11. **End-of-life checkpoint.** Drafted as one provision conditioning unsupervised end-of-life practice, not certification. Right level?
12. **PTSD.** Hours not drafted, because the trauma module is expressly not PTSD treatment training. The collaboration rule belongs in 7.35.3.13 B, outside this scope. Pursue separately, and is it "recommended" or "required"?

## For counsel, not for her

13. **Does 7.35.2.7 NMAC define practitioner and facilitator as species of the statutory "clinician"?** If not, 7.35.3.14 (B) and (C) may reach further than Section 5 of the Act does. One document answers it. See `blocking-defects.md`, "The statutory frame," item 3.
