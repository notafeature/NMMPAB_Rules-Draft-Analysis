Published text

Provision by provision

7.35.3.19 (A)

Hour totals, and when the practicum may start

DeferredNew gate
An individual who seeks to become certified as a practitioner or facilitator shall participate in supervised practice training, otherwise referred to as a “practicum”, after completing at least half of the didactic requirements and all of the simulated patient requirements of the educational requirements. The practicum shall consist of a minimum of 100 hours of supervised practice training for facilitators and 120 hours for practitioners and shall be completed prior to applying for certification. Students shall participate in a minimum of 80 hours of administration day sessions… with a minimum of 14 different patients over a minimum of eight different administration and same-day sessions.

Sets the totals, and bars a student from starting until half the classroom hours and all simulated patient hours are done. The gate is new in this draft, has no counterpart in the July 9 draft, and appears in neither July 17 transcript.

Published rule, p. 12 · 7.35.3.19 on the published rule page

7.35.3.19 (B)

Preparation and integration hours

Deferred
Students shall participate in a minimum of 20 hours of in-person preparation and integration sessions… This shall include a minimum of six different patients during individual sessions. This shall include at least one group preparatory and one group integration session with a minimum of four or more patients.

The 20 hours here plus the 80 in (A) make up the 100-hour facilitator total.

Published rule, p. 13 · 7.35.3.19 on the published rule page

7.35.3.19 (C)

Practitioner supervisory hours

Deferred
Practitioners shall complete an additional minimum of 20 hours as a practitioner supervising facilitators during in-person administration day sessions, which shall include a minimum of two different patients during individual administration day sessions, and a minimum of one group administration day sessions with a minimum of four or more patients in the group.

These 20 hours are the difference between the facilitator total of 100 and the practitioner total of 120. The board named them in the deferral motion.

Published rule, p. 13 · 7.35.3.19 on the published rule page

7.35.3.19 (D)

Where the practicum may happen

Dependency
All practicum hours shall take place in an approved healing center or other approved location.

Ties every practicum hour to a facility that must first be certified under 7.35.3.11. Any change to placement, including a site-supervisor or apprenticeship model, changes this sentence.

Published rule, p. 13 · 7.35.3.19 on the published rule page

7.35.3.19 (F)

Departmental discretion to waive or reduce

Deferred
The department may otherwise waive, temporarily suspend, or reduce the practicum requirements for individuals applying for certification, in order to facilitate the certification of individuals trained by other government-approved programs, and to build the initial infrastructure of the program.

Open-ended and undated. No standard governs when it applies, and nothing ends it.

Published rule, p. 13 · 7.35.3.19 on the published rule page

7.35.3.19 (G)

In-state waiver, 40 contact hours

Conflicts
An applicant for certification shall not be required to satisfy the full New Mexico practicum hours requirement if the applicant: (1) Applies for certification by December 31, 2027; (2) Completes the didactic requirements by December 31, 2027; (3) Graduates from an educational program that the department certifies by December 31, 2027 or that the department has included on the department-approved list…; and (4) Demonstrates completion of at least 40 hours of contact time… including: (a) A minimum of two separate individual sessions… and (b) A minimum of one group session…

Requires two individual sessions and one group session. The out-of-jurisdiction waiver at 7.35.3.10 (D)(1) requires two individual and two group for the same 40 hours. Both cannot be the standard.

Published rule, p. 13 · 7.35.3.19 on the published rule page

7.35.3.18 (C)

Didactic hours the practicum gates on

Raised 25 to 30
All practitioners and facilitators shall complete a psilocybin therapy module consisting of a minimum of 30 didactic hours and 5 hours of simulated patient experience, prior to applying for certification.

7.35.3.19 (A) gates practicum entry on "half of the didactic requirements and all of the simulated patient requirements," so this figure sets the gate. It was covered by the same 7-0 deferral vote and was published five hours higher than it stood that day. Three subjects were added: Trauma-Informed Care, Dosing, and Non-ordinary states of consciousness.

Published rule, p. 11 · 7.35.3.18 on the published rule page

7.35.3.14

Who may possess and administer psilocybin

Students absent
Certification of a practitioner, facilitator, or healing center shall enable practitioners, facilitators, and owners and employees of healing centers to do the following… (C) Healing centers: Owners and employees of healing centers who are registered with the department may purchase… possess… and may sell or otherwise administer those products to qualified patients…

This is the only grant of authority to possess or administer psilocybin in the rule. It names three categories and a practicum student is none of them, because certification is what the practicum is a prerequisite for. Meanwhile 7.35.3.19 (A) requires students to participate in 80 hours of administration day sessions, where they are provided the opportunity to "experience, observe, or conduct supervised facilitation" of those sessions.

The phrase "registered with the department" appears exactly once in all nineteen pages, in this subsection. Nothing in the rule creates that registration.

Published rule, p. 9 · 7.35.3.14 on the published rule page

7.35.3.20 (H)(5)

Staffing ratio counting students

Depends on .14
…in group administration sessions a minimum of one practitioner for every eight patients and a minimum of one facilitator or qualified student for every two patients. For purposes of the foregoing provision, a student shall be deemed qualified if they are registered with a certified educational program and they have completed at least 50 hours of their practicum.

Counts students toward mandatory coverage, which means a session can lawfully rely on someone 7.35.3.14 does not authorize to handle the medicine. New in this draft. Denali Wilson raised the idea at the July 17 committee meeting and the department said it would take it back.

Published rule, p. 14 · 7.35.3.20 on the published rule page

7.35.3.10 (D)(1)

Out-of-jurisdiction waiver, 40 contact hours

Conflicts with 19(G)
An applicant for certification as a practitioner or facilitator on the basis of an educational program from another jurisdiction, whose application is received by the department by December 31, 2027, shall not be required to complete the full practicum hours required by New Mexico, but shall demonstrate completion of at least 40 hours of contact time with a minimum of two separate individual sessions and two separate group sessions…

A second practicum waiver, sitting outside 7.35.3.19 in the out-of-jurisdiction application section. Same 40 hours, different session test. Amending 19(G) alone leaves this standing.

Published rule, p. 5 · 7.35.3.10 on the published rule page

7.35.3.11

Creates the approved locations 19(D) requires

Dependency
An applicant seeking certification as a healing center shall submit a complete application through the electronic system designated by the department…

Every practicum hour must occur at a location certified under this section. No certified locations, no practicum. Any placement model that decouples the practicum from healing centers has to be written here as well as in 19(D).

Published rule, p. 5 · 7.35.3.11 on the published rule page

New section

Authority for a student to handle psilocybin

Does not exist

The rule places students in administration sessions with the opportunity to conduct supervised facilitation, counts them toward staffing, and never authorizes them to possess or administer the medicine. The gap has to be closed by adding to 7.35.3.14, or by creating a training permit as its own section.

On July 17 the department said a training permit was unnecessary because students "already will have that kind of protection ability to be there as a part of being registered as a student with an educational program." No such provision was published.

The rule currently ends at 7.35.3.28, Severability. A new section numbered 29 would sit after it, which is unusual drafting; placing it in the training run is cleaner.

7.35.3.14 on the published rule page

New subsection

The healing-center registration 14(C) requires

Does not exist

7.35.3.14 (C) conditions the authority of healing-center owners and employees on being "registered with the department." No section creates that registration, so on the face of the rule no owner or employee qualifies. It belongs in 7.35.3.11, which handles healing-center certification, or as a new subsection of 7.35.3.20.

The document this page is read from

August 25, 2026

Revised proposed ruleCurrent state of truth

The revised proposed rule, published August 25 with the notice fixing the October 2 hearing, superseding the set-aside July 23 text. Raised the practitioner and facilitator therapy module from 30 didactic hours to 65, at least one third in person, and the simulated patient requirement from 5 hours to 10. Added eleven curriculum topics without per-area hour minimums, a case-presentation evaluation inside the unchanged 100 and 120 practicum hours, a low-risk requirement on the first 20 administration-day hours, and a didactic waiver. Kept the role names certifying clinician and practitioner, defining them in amendments to 7.35.2.7 published the same day.

This document supersedes four earlier ones. Each of them, what it is, what superseded it and when, and a download, is in the register at Meetings and filings.
