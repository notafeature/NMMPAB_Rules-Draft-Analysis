The deferred training requirements

Updated July 25, 2026.

On July 17 the Advisory Board voted seven to nothing to send the practicum and didactic hour requirements back to the Training and Education Committee. This page follows those requirements alone, from the committee's original recommendation through to the rule the department published on July 23, so that the effect of the deferral can be read directly rather than inferred.

How to read the three columns

A requirement on this page has passed through three documents. Each column shows what that document said, and the two arrows between them show what changed at each step.

The deferred set

Requirement by requirement

These are the requirements covered by the July 17 deferral, together with the two provisions they depend on. The committee's recommendation on them is being prepared and had not reached the department before publication.

Total practicum hours

Deferred July 17, published unchanged

One hundred hours for facilitators and one hundred and twenty for practitioners. The figure originated in the committee's recommendation and has not moved since, through two subsequent drafts and a unanimous vote to reconsider it.

Recommended June 12

"must include a minimum of one hundred (100) hours of supervised practice training for Facilitators and one hundred and twenty (120) hours for Practitioners"

Recommendation, p. 7

Carried overHour totals identical. The draft adds that the practicum must be completed before applying for certification.

Drafted July 9

"The practicum shall consist of a minimum of 100 hours of supervised practice training for facilitators and 120 hours for practitioners and must be completed prior to applying for certification."

July 9 draft, p. 5

Carried overOne word changed, "must" to "shall". The hour totals are untouched.

Published July 23

"The practicum shall consist of a minimum of 100 hours of supervised practice training for facilitators and 120 hours for practitioners and shall be completed prior to applying for certification."

7.35.3.19 (A), p. 12

The board voted seven to nothing on July 17 to send this figure to the Training and Education Committee for reassessment. The department published it unchanged six days later, and has said on the record that it remains open to adjusting the hours.

Shared didactic module

Deferred July 17, published higher

The classroom requirement shared by practitioners and facilitators rose from twenty-five hours to thirty in the published rule, and three subjects were added to the required curriculum. This figure was covered by the same deferral vote as the practicum hours.

Recommended June 12

The recommendation set the curriculum by subject rather than by a single shared hour total. The twenty-five hour figure first appears in the department's drafting.

Recommendation

ChangedThe department consolidated the subjects into a single module and set it at twenty-five hours, with five hours of simulated patient experience on top.

Drafted July 9

"All practitioners and facilitators shall complete a psilocybin therapy module consisting of a minimum of 25 didactic hours, prior to applying for certification"

July 9 draft, p. 4

RaisedTwenty-five hours became thirty. Three subjects were added: Trauma-Informed Care, Dosing, and Non-ordinary states of consciousness. The required list grew from fourteen items to seventeen.

Published July 23

"All practitioners and facilitators shall complete a psilocybin therapy module consisting of a minimum of 30 didactic hours and 5 hours of simulated patient experience, prior to applying for certification"

7.35.3.18 (C), p. 11

This is the one deferred figure that moved. It was published five hours higher than it stood on the day the board voted to send it to committee. Adding Trauma-Informed Care answers a recommendation Dr. Anne Metz made to the committee on July 17, which asked for trauma to be taught as a subject in its own right rather than folded into other modules.

When the practicum may begin

New in the published rule

The published rule bars a student from starting the practicum until half the classroom hours and all of the simulated patient hours are finished. Neither earlier document contains this condition, and it was not raised at either July 17 meeting.

Recommended June 12

No counterpart. The recommendation sets no sequencing condition between the classroom hours and the practicum.

No counterpart

Drafted July 9

No counterpart. The July 9 draft sets no sequencing condition.

AddedA new condition appears, gating entry to the practicum on classroom and simulated patient progress.

Published July 23

"shall participate in supervised practice training, otherwise referred to as a ‘practicum’, after completing at least half of the didactic requirements and all of the simulated patient requirements of the educational requirements"

7.35.3.19 (A), p. 12

The committee discussed the order of classroom and practical work on July 17 and reached no conclusion. This condition appears in neither transcript, and it changes what a student must do before entering a placement.

Practitioner supervisory hours

Deferred July 17, published unchanged

Practitioners owe twenty hours supervising facilitators, on top of the practicum itself. The board named this figure specifically when it sent the hours to committee.

Recommended June 12

"Practitioners must complete an additional minimum of twenty (20) hours as a practitioner supervising facilitators…"

Recommendation, p. 8

Carried overUnchanged in substance.

Drafted July 9

Twenty hours supervising facilitators during administration and same-day therapy sessions, with minimums for individual and group sessions.

July 9 draft, p. 5

Carried overThe hours are unchanged. "Same-day therapy" becomes "administration day", part of a wider removal of therapy language from the rule.

Published July 23

"Practitioners shall complete an additional minimum of 20 hours as a practitioner supervising facilitators during in-person administration day sessions"

7.35.3.19 (C), p. 13

The chair named the twenty supervisory hours in the motion putting the deferral to a vote. The figure is unchanged in the published rule.

Still to be added to this page

The remaining deferred and dependent provisions follow the same three-column form: the composition of the practicum hours, the practicum location requirement, the two waiver routes and the dates attached to them, the mentoring requirement, continuing education, and the staffing ratio that counts students toward required coverage.

Scope

What this page covers, and what it does not

This page is deliberately narrow. It follows only the requirements the Advisory Board deferred on July 17, together with the provisions those requirements depend on, so that a reader can see the effect of the deferral without reading around it.

The complete proposed rule runs to twenty-eight sections and covers patients, healing centers, product storage, adverse event reporting, and discipline as well as training. The section-by-section record of the whole document is on the draft page. What the published rule requires of each role, and what it costs, is on Training hours. The dated record of how the requirements reached this point is on History.

Provenance: the document chain and this page’s revisions▸

The document chain, oldest first

  1. June 12, 2026

    Committee recommendation

    The Training and Education Committee's own recommendation. Origin of the 100 and 120 practicum hours and the 20 supervisory hours. The Advisory Board voted 3-2 on June 26 to accept it, setting three items aside.

  2. June 25, 2026

    Department draft

    The department's first rules-style draft of the same subject. Introduced the module test-out mechanism that every later draft keeps, and the first reciprocity provision, which the June 12 recommendation did not contain.

  3. July 9, 2026

    Board-meeting draft

    The draft the Advisory Board went through section by section on July 9. Used placeholder section numbers shown as 7.35.3.X. Set the shared didactic module at 25 hours. Carried the older waiver dates of December 31, 2026 and June 30, 2027, which the board extended by vote that day without the text being updated.

  4. July 23, 2026

    Published proposed ruleCurrent state of truth

    The department's published proposed rule, and the text going to the August 28 hearing. First version with final section numbering, 7.35.3.1 through .28. Raised the shared didactic module from 25 hours to 30 and added three curriculum topics. Carried the 100 and 120 practicum hours forward unchanged, six days after the board voted 7-0 to send those hours back to committee. Wrote both waiver dates as December 31, 2027. Added a rule that the practicum may not begin before half the didactic and all simulated patient hours are complete.
