How to read the labels

  • Current The version in force for citation purposes right now.
  • Superseded Replaced by a later version. Kept because the site's history pages cite it.
  • Record A meeting record or a submission. Not superseded by anything; it is what it is.

Meetings

Transcripts

Transcripts of the meetings this site quotes. All are unofficial. The Department of Health posts its own recordings and minutes, and those govern. Read the note on each before relying on a line.

Two kinds of transcript here, and the difference matters

The July 9 transcript is speaker-tagged: each passage is labeled with who is speaking. The two July 17 transcripts are not. They were captured from auto-generated meeting notes that carry no speaker labels, and none have been added.

Where this site quotes a July 17 transcript and names a speaker, the speaker is fixed by the surrounding text, for example someone being thanked by name immediately after speaking, and the basis is recorded. Where context does not fix a speaker, no name is given. Automatic transcription also misreads names, numbers, and technical terms, and garbles speech during crosstalk.
