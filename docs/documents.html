Meetings

Transcripts

Transcripts of the meetings this site quotes. All are unofficial. The Department of Health posts its own recordings and minutes, and those govern. Read the note on each before relying on a line.

Two kinds of transcript here, and the difference matters

The July 9 transcript is speaker-tagged: each passage is labeled with who is speaking. The two July 17 transcripts are not. They were captured from auto-generated meeting notes that carry no speaker labels, and none have been added.

Where this site quotes a July 17 transcript and names a speaker, the speaker is fixed by the surrounding text, for example someone being thanked by name immediately after speaking, and the basis is recorded. Where context does not fix a speaker, no name is given. Automatic transcription also misreads names, numbers, and technical terms, and garbles speech during crosstalk.

Provenance: the document chain and this page’s revisions▸

The document chain, oldest first

  1. June 12, 2026

    Committee recommendation

    The Training and Education Committee's own recommendation. Origin of the 100 and 120 practicum hours and the 20 supervisory hours. The Advisory Board voted 3-2 on June 26 to accept it, setting three items aside.

  2. June 25, 2026

    Department draft

    The department's first rules-style draft of the same subject. Introduced the module test-out mechanism that every later draft keeps, and the first reciprocity provision, which the June 12 recommendation did not contain.

  3. July 9, 2026

    Board-meeting draft

    The draft the Advisory Board went through section by section on July 9. Used placeholder section numbers shown as 7.35.3.X. Set the shared didactic module at 25 hours. Carried the older waiver dates of December 31, 2026 and June 30, 2027, which the board extended by vote that day without the text being updated.

  4. July 23, 2026

    Published proposed ruleCurrent state of truth

    The department's published proposed rule, and the text going to the August 28 hearing. First version with final section numbering, 7.35.3.1 through .28. Raised the shared didactic module from 25 hours to 30 and added three curriculum topics. Carried the 100 and 120 practicum hours forward unchanged, six days after the board voted 7-0 to send those hours back to committee. Wrote both waiver dates as December 31, 2027. Added a rule that the practicum may not begin before half the didactic and all simulated patient hours are complete.
