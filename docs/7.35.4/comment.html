Proposed October 20 · rule hearing November 20, by notice

Comment

The proposed 7.35.4 NMAC and the proposed amendment to 7.35.2.7 NMAC go to a rule hearing on November 20, 2026, fixed by the notice published October 20 with them. Comment is taken orally at the hearing, in person, by video conference, or by telephone, and in writing until the hearing closes. This page holds the official channel's mechanics, what is at issue, and the community input channel this site operates.

Created October 22, 2026.

The official channel

The rule hearing is Friday, November 20, 2026 at 9:00 AM, in the Harold Runnels Building auditorium, 1190 St. Francis Drive, Santa Fe, fixed by the notice published October 20. It is also held by Microsoft Teams video conference and by telephone; the notice carries the meeting code, the passcode, the telephone number, and a registration link for a calendar invitation. Any member of the public may attend and give oral comment, and all comments are recorded. Written comment goes to Jacob Clark of the department's office of general counsel, by e-mail to jacob.clark@doh.nm.gov or by U.S. postal mail to NMDOH OGC, P.O. Box 26110, 1190 St. Francis Dr., Suite N-4095, Santa Fe, NM 87502-6110, and must be received by the close of the hearing. The department publishes written comments on its website within three days of receipt. An accommodation request goes to Jacob Clark at (505) 827-2997, with ten days' advance notice requested. Notice, pp. 1 to 2

What is at issue

The notice lists what the rule includes: eligibility and submittal requirements for patients, covered medical services, claim and invoice requirements, reimbursement, record retention and audit, permitted travel, program monitoring, grounds for denial, holds, and suspensions, recovery of overpayments, and review of adverse determinations. Notice, p. 1

.14Session count. Two preparatory or integrative sessions with each later administration, where the board voted three. The comparison.
.14What is covered. The diagnostic evaluation and the medication are not named among allowable expenses. The comparison.
.10Other payers first. Medicaid named among the payers a claimant must pursue before the fund. The comparison.
2.7Definitions. Eleven terms added to 7.35.2.7 for this Part, among them allowable amount, claimant, covered treatment, household, and other payer. Noted at 7.35.4.7.

The input form

Recommendations and resources sent through this form are compiled and forwarded to the Department of Health as one package, and may be shared with a board member who asks to receive them. The form asks for one thing: a short description of what you are sending. Contact details are optional.

How submissions are used

Submissions are reviewed before anything is passed along or published. Anything published on this site is de-identified unless you checked the box above. Publication of a comment records it rather than endorses it. Your email is used only to follow up on what you sent. No submission concerning this Part has been received through the form.
