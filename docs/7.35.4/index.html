New Mexico medical psilocybin program · 7.35.4 NMAC

Where things stand

Created October 22, 2026. Every claim on this page cites the proposed text, the hearing notice, or a department recording.

New Mexico's rule for the medical psilocybin treatment equity fund, 7.35.4 NMAC, is proposed, not in effect. The Department of Health published it on October 20, 2026 with an amendment to the definitions at 7.35.2.7 NMAC, and fixed a rule hearing on both for November 20, 2026. The fund itself is created by the Medical Psilocybin Act, Section 26-2D-11 NMSA 1978, for treatment of qualified patients who meet income requirements set by department rule; this is that rule. It follows a recommendation the Advisory Board voted on September 11, and departs from it in places. This Part of the site holds the proposed text, what it would require of the people it reaches, the recommendation beside it, and the record.

Where things stand

OPENThe department published the proposed 7.35.4 NMAC, Treatment Equity Fund, on October 20, 2026, with a proposed amendment to the definitions at 7.35.2.7 NMAC and a notice fixing the rule hearing for November 20, 2026, 9:00 AM, in the Harold Runnels Building auditorium in Santa Fe, in person, by video conference, and by telephone. The rule is proposed and not in effect. Written comment must be received by the close of the hearing; the notice carries the video conference code, the telephone number, and the written comment address. The text is on The proposed rule.
OPENThe Advisory Board voted 7-0 on September 11, 2026 to recommend the equity fund framework from the Equity, Access and Cultural Considerations Committee, with three preparatory or integrative sessions for each additional administration session. The proposed 7.35.4.14 (A) allows two. The diagnostic evaluation and the cost of medication, both voted, are not named in the section that lists allowable expenses. Where the text departs from the board vote

.14 (A)Two preparatory or integrative sessions with each later administration; the board voted three. The comparison.
.14 (A)The diagnostic evaluation, voted by both bodies, is not named. The comparison.
.10 (B)Medicaid named among the payers to pursue first, the language the board left to the attorneys. The comparison.
NEWTen provisions the department introduced that neither the committee nor the board took up, among them a 12-month limit on eligibility, a 180-day claim deadline, and no review of a denial made for lack of funds. Each is marked New from the department where it sits. The list.
