The board recommendation

On August 18, 2026 the Equity, Access and Cultural Considerations Committee voted 5-0 to send an equity fund framework to the Medical Psilocybin Advisory Board. On September 11 the board voted 7-0 to recommend it to the department, with one change: three preparatory or integrative sessions for each administration after the first, where the committee had two. The department published the proposed rule on October 20. This page sets each voted item beside the proposed text and states the result. It is committee material: the recommendation does not bind the department, and the proposed text is not in effect.

Created October 22, 2026.

The proposed rule, 7.35.4 NMACCurrent state of truth

The proposed rule, published by the department on October 20, 2026: twenty sections setting patient eligibility for the medical psilocybin treatment equity fund, the application, reimbursement and claims, allowable expenses and travel, records, audits, payment denial and suspension, overpayments, program monitoring, and administrative review. It is the first published text of this Part, and it is proposed, not in effect; the rule hearing is November 20, 2026.
