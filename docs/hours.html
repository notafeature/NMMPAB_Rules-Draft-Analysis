As published: 100 supervised hours for facilitators and 120 for practitioners, every hour inside an approved healing center or other approved location, completed before applying for certification.

Published figures: 7.35.3.18 and 7.35.3.19, published July 23, 2026, pages 11 to 13 of the official PDF. Recommended figures: the summary of the recommended rules, submitted July 27, 2026, stated in full on the recommendation page; a staged practicum of 60 hours for facilitators and 70 for licensed providers, 80 didactic hours, and 20 hours of case presentation and consultation tied to two presented cases. The cost estimate and the department's willingness to adjust the lengths of time: the July 17 committee meeting, as recorded in the transcript. The full pre-redesign hours page, with the July 9 and July 17 records, benchmarks, and cost variables, is kept at training-hours-record.html. Hours toward a specialized domain, discussed at the End-of-Life Care committee on July 16, would sit on top of these totals as an addendum; the proposed ranges are on the specialized-domains page.
