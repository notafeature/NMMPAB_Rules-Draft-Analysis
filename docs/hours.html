The strip below the role always names the position you are looking at. Hours in supervised practice at $150 to $200 an hour, the estimate given to the committee on July 17. Tuition, supervisor fees, and travel come on top.
In the recommendation, a facilitator completes 80 classroom hours with minimums in nine content areas, then a staged practicum of 102 hours: 24 with well participants, 24 co-facilitating low-acuity patients, 12 of group work, and 42 of supervised practice on two cases, whose 18-hour case presentation and consultation group sits inside the total. Nothing further is required after the practicum; compensation is permitted from the group-work stage.

The recommendation's figures are reported, not checked. They are sourced to the document shown and read aloud at the August 21 committee meeting; no recording or transcript of that meeting is held on this site yet, an absence tracked in the gaps register. When the department posts its recording, those figures are checked against it. The last published text's figures are cited to the rule published July 23, 2026, held on this site.

Where these hours sit in a route to a permit is on Routes to a permit. Which licenses qualify for each permit is on Which licenses qualify.

What the classroom figure counts. Classroom here is the module total, the didactic hours and the simulated patient experience added together, because that is the only basis on which all three positions can be compared. In the published rule that is 40 hours: 7.35.3.18 (C), p. 11, sets "a minimum of 30 didactic hours and 5 hours of simulated patient experience", and (D) for facilitators or (E) for practitioners adds a role module of "a minimum of five didactic hours". Didactic alone is therefore 35 and the module total is 40. The New Mexico module at 7.35.3.18 (A) is required of every role and carries no hour count anywhere in the rule, so it adds nothing to either figure.

The last published text. Supervised practice at 7.35.3.19 (A), pp. 12 to 13, which sets "a minimum of 100 hours of supervised practice training for facilitators and 120 hours for practitioners". The practitioner's 20 hours supervising facilitators, at 7.35.3.19 (C), p. 13, sit inside that 120 rather than adding to it. Mentoring at 7.35.3.17 (A), p. 10, which requires "a minimum of 10 hours of mentoring sessions after graduation and after practicum hours are completed." All of it in the rule published July 23, 2026.

The recommendation. The committee's August 21 position, stated in full on the recommendation page: 80 didactic hours with minimums in nine content areas summing to 63, a staged practicum of 102 hours for facilitators and 114 for licensed providers, and an 18-hour case presentation and consultation group inside the practicum total, on two cases the student personally provided. Its 80 counts the 2 simulated-patient hours inside the total exactly as the published 40 does, so the figures compare directly. Nothing further is required after the practicum, so the third quantity is zero for this position. Earlier positions: Dr. Anne Metz's July 17 presentation is on the training-hours record with its documents; the committee's July 27 submission, 80 classroom hours with a staged practicum of 60 or 70, is kept in the recommendation's history on the recommendation page.

The cost estimate and the department's willingness to adjust the lengths of time: the July 17 committee meeting, as recorded in the transcript. The full pre-redesign hours page, with the July 9 and July 17 records, benchmarks, and cost variables, is kept at training-hours-record.html. Hours toward a specialized domain, discussed at the End-of-Life Care committee on July 16, would sit on top of these totals as an addendum; the proposed ranges are on the specialized-domains page. Nothing here is final rule text.

The document this page is read from

August 25, 2026

Revised proposed ruleCurrent state of truth

The revised proposed rule, published August 25 with the notice fixing the October 2 hearing, superseding the set-aside July 23 text. Raised the practitioner and facilitator therapy module from 30 didactic hours to 65, at least one third in person, and the simulated patient requirement from 5 hours to 10. Added eleven curriculum topics without per-area hour minimums, a case-presentation evaluation inside the unchanged 100 and 120 practicum hours, a low-risk requirement on the first 20 administration-day hours, and a didactic waiver. Kept the role names certifying clinician and practitioner, defining them in amendments to 7.35.2.7 published the same day.

This document supersedes four earlier ones. Each of them, what it is, what superseded it and when, and a download, is in the register at Meetings and filings.
