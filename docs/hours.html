As published, a facilitator completes 40 classroom hours, then 100 hours of supervised practice, every hour of it inside an approved healing center or other approved location and finished before applying for certification, then 10 hours of mentoring with the program faculty.

These three figures are reported, not checked. The summary of the recommended rules submitted on July 27 is not held in this repository and is not in the public record, so nothing here can be compared against it. The figures Dr. Anne Metz presented on July 17 can be checked, against the document linked below.

Where these hours sit in a route to a permit is on Routes to a permit. Which licenses qualify for each permit is on Which licenses qualify.

What the classroom figure counts. Classroom here is the module total, the didactic hours and the simulated patient experience added together, because that is the only basis on which all three positions can be compared. In the published rule that is 40 hours: 7.35.3.18 (C), p. 11, sets "a minimum of 30 didactic hours and 5 hours of simulated patient experience", and (D) for facilitators or (E) for practitioners adds a role module of "a minimum of five didactic hours". Didactic alone is therefore 35 and the module total is 40. The New Mexico module at 7.35.3.18 (A) is required of every role and carries no hour count anywhere in the rule, so it adds nothing to either figure.

The published rule. Supervised practice at 7.35.3.19 (A), pp. 12 to 13, which sets "a minimum of 100 hours of supervised practice training for facilitators and 120 hours for practitioners". The practitioner's 20 hours supervising facilitators, at 7.35.3.19 (C), p. 13, sit inside that 120 rather than adding to it. Mentoring at 7.35.3.17 (A), p. 10, which requires "a minimum of 10 hours of mentoring sessions after graduation and after practicum hours are completed." All of it in the rule published July 23, 2026.

Dr. Anne Metz's recommendation. Her written recommendations of July 17, 2026, which this repository holds. Recommendation 2, page 1, adopts "a single state-level standard of 84 didactic hours for both Facilitator and Licensed Provider permits"; the curriculum table on pages 2 and 3 totals 84 across ten content areas and states that "Component ranges are illustrative; the binding minimum is the total." Recommendation 3, pages 3 and 4, sets four practicum steps of approximately 30, 20, and 12 hours, plus 10 supervisory hours for licensed providers only, giving "approximately 62 hours for Facilitators (steps 1–3); approximately 72 hours for Licensed Providers (steps 1–4)." Recommendation 4, pages 4 and 5, sets "20–30 total hours of supervision or consultation completed during the training permit period, in addition to the practicum hours above"; the model shows the low end, following the convention on this site that where a source gives a range the low end is used and the range is stated. Because those consultation hours sit on top of the practicum, the program total shown for this position is 84 plus the practicum plus 20.

The committee recommendation. The summary of the recommended rules, submitted to the department on July 27, 2026, stated in full on the recommendation page: 80 classroom hours, a staged practicum of 60 hours for facilitators and 70 for licensed providers, and 20 hours of case presentation and consultation tied to two presented cases. Its 80 is stated across nine content areas running from 20 hours in core psychotherapy skills and ethics down to 2 hours in simulated patient work, so it counts simulated patient hours inside the total exactly as the published 40 does, and the two figures compare directly. The submission calls the 80 a didactic standard; on this site it is the classroom module total, which is the same quantity. The document itself is not held in this repository and is not in the public record, so these figures are stated as reported rather than checked against a source.

The cost estimate and the department's willingness to adjust the lengths of time: the July 17 committee meeting, as recorded in the transcript. The full pre-redesign hours page, with the July 9 and July 17 records, benchmarks, and cost variables, is kept at training-hours-record.html. Hours toward a specialized domain, discussed at the End-of-Life Care committee on July 16, would sit on top of these totals as an addendum; the proposed ranges are on the specialized-domains page. Nothing here is final rule text.

July 23, 2026

Published proposed ruleCurrent state of truth

The department's published proposed rule, and the operative text; the department stated on August 21 that revised rules publish August 25, with the hearing, first set for August 28, anticipated for October 2. First version with final section numbering, 7.35.3.1 through .28. Raised the shared didactic module from 25 hours to 30 and added three curriculum topics. Carried the 100 and 120 practicum hours forward unchanged, six days after the board voted 7-0 to send those hours back to committee. Wrote both waiver dates as December 31, 2027. Added a rule that the practicum may not begin before half the didactic and all simulated patient hours are complete.

This document supersedes three earlier ones. Each of them, what it is, what superseded it and when, and a download, is in the register at Meetings and filings.
