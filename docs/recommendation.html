The committee recommendation

On July 17 the Advisory Board voted 7-0 to send the practicum and didactic hours, with the definitions, to the Training and Education Committee for a recommendation. On July 27 the recommendation went to the department as a summary of the recommended rules. This page holds what it recommends and what it would change beside what the published text says.

Updated July 27, 2026.

The hours after the practicum are mentoring in the published text, at 7.35.3.17 (A), and case presentation and consultation in the recommendation. The recommendation moves hours from supervised practice into the classroom; the program totals barely move, and the supervised hours, the costly ones, fall by two fifths. The working model of the hours lets you move the quantities yourself and see what changes for the person doing the training.

What the submission recommends

The summary of the recommended rules, dated July 2026, states the education and training requirements in plain language. Its figures are stated here as fact about what is recommended.

NAME"Practitioner" becomes "Licensed Provider": a professional holding a license to deliver medical, counseling, mental health, or behavioral health care.
MODULEA New Mexico module for every role, with no exemption by reciprocity, ending in a competency evaluation.
80A single didactic standard of 80 hours for Licensed Providers and Facilitators, with minimum hours in nine content areas, from 20 in core psychotherapy skills and ethics to 2 in simulated patient work. Certifying clinicians complete 8.
60 / 70A staged practicum of 60 hours for Facilitators and 70 for Licensed Providers: well participants first, then co-facilitation with a department-permitted Licensed Provider, then group work, with every stage spanning preparation, administration, and integration. After 50 hours a student may see high-acuity patients when accompanied by a Licensed Provider.
20After the practicum, 20 hours of case presentation and consultation in which the student presents two cases they personally provided, evaluated before independent practice.
2027A transition pathway: applicants who complete their didactic requirements by December 31, 2027 may satisfy a reduced practicum of 40 contact hours, or an approved out-of-state practicum.

Source: the summary of the recommended rules, submitted July 27, 2026, five pages. The document is not published on this site; its figures are stated above in full.

The basis: the July 17 presentation

Dr. Anne Metz, a clinical facilitator who has worked in the Oregon and Colorado programs, presented four recommendations to the committee on July 17. The submission carries their structure, with the figures settled: the 84 didactic hours became 80, the 62 to 72 practicum hours became 60 and 70, and the 20 to 30 consultation hours became 20.

1Rename "practitioner" to "licensed provider." The current word says nothing about training and collides with "nurse practitioner."
2A single 84-hour didactic standard, applied and practice-based, with a module-by-module test-out except for the New Mexico module.
3A staged practicum of 62 to 72 hours in four steps, from well participants to lead-chair hours, opening a lawful path to client contact during training.
4Supervision of 20 to 30 hours tied to two formally presented cases, so sign-off requires having actually seen clients.

Source: the July 17 committee transcript and her written recommendations. A drafting folder holding the working redline, with its own audit trail, is in the repository behind this site.

Also raised, not in the published rule

The End-of-Life committee raised specialization on July 16: an endorsement layered on a core permit for specific practice settings, with end-of-life care as the worked example. Nothing in the published text creates a specialization. The material is recorded on the record page and in the July 16 meeting sources.
