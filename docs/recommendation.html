The committee recommendation

On July 17 the Advisory Board voted 7-0 to send the practicum and didactic hours, with the definitions, to the Training and Education Committee. The committee submitted a summary of the recommended rules on July 27 and refined its position at the August 21 committee meeting, where the position was shown beside the department's own proposal. No vote was taken; the chair held one off deliberately with the two proposals close, and the position is in the department's hands for the revised rules. This page holds the August 21 position in full, beside the department's proposal and the last published text.

Updated August 24, 2026.

Status

NOWThe recommendation stands at the August 21 position below. The July 23 publication and its August 28 hearing were set aside; no proposed rule is currently published.
THENThe department writes revised proposed rules, with August 25 its stated date, presents them to the board, and holds the rule hearing, anticipated for early October. When the revised text appears, this site reviews it against this position, provision by provision.

The pathway, in one picture

The three roles

CCThe certifying clinician performs medical screening and clearance: that the diagnosis exists and that psilocybin treatment is medically appropriate. The department confirmed on August 21 that counseling-side appropriateness sits with the licensed provider.
LP"Practitioner" becomes "Licensed Provider": a professional holding a license to deliver medical, counseling, mental health, or behavioral health care. The department used the committee's term at the August 21 meeting.
FThe facilitator supports patients through psilocybin sessions in a non-clinical role.

Both August 21 proposals double the didactic hours of the last published text and land within twenty hours of each other in total. The practicum difference is structural: the committee stages it and separates the 18-hour consultation; the department reaches its totals through a general practicum of six individual patients and two groups, folding case presentation into the practicum hours. The working model of the hours lets you move the quantities and see what changes for the person doing the training.

The didactic: 80 hours

MODULEA New Mexico module for every role, with no exemption by reciprocity, covering the Act, the medical and therapeutic model, social, cultural, and historical aspects of psilocybin use including traditional use by Native American and other populations in New Mexico, spiritual, existential, religious, and theological aspects, and the state research and data collection requirements, ending in a competency evaluation.
8Certifying clinicians complete a module of at least eight didactic hours: pharmacology and interactions, the role's responsibilities, 42 CFR Part 2, diagnosis of qualifying conditions, medical clearance, psychedelic emergencies and monitoring, and challenging experiences including hallucinogen persisting perception disorder.
80Licensed Providers and Facilitators complete at least 80 didactic hours, below Oregon's 120 and Colorado's 150; the position states the calibration is deliberate, since graduates in both states report longer didactic requirements did not buy greater depth.

The remaining 17 hours go to the topics required without an hour minimum: an overview of Licensed Provider and Facilitator responsibilities, including care-team work and the non-clinical peer support facilitator role, and treatment models. The position notes that the seven trauma hours are not PTSD treatment training and should not be represented as such; where PTSD treatment falls outside a provider's scope, collaboration with a PTSD-trained provider is the appropriate path. Whether these per-area minimums belong in the rule or in guidance documents the rule points to was argued at the August 21 meeting and not resolved; the department recommends content areas in the rule without per-area hours.

The staged practicum: 102 or 114 supervised hours

Supervised practice is sequenced from lower-risk to higher-risk experience, and no one completes it without real client contact. Every hour is supervised, every stage spans preparation, administration, and integration, and hours earned simultaneously count once toward the total. All practicum hours take place in an approved healing center or other approved location.

The facilitator total is 24 + 24 + 12 + 42 = 102; the Licensed Provider total adds the 12 supervisory hours for 114. Once a student completes 48 hours, the end of stage two, they are qualified to provide care to high-acuity patients when accompanied by a Licensed Provider: a graduated on-ramp that expands what a student can do as demonstrated experience accumulates.

Case presentation and consultation: the checkpoint

Every student participates in at least 18 hours of case presentation and consultation with educational program faculty, which programs may provide through a contracted third party. Each student presents at least two cases in which they personally provided direct psilocybin services: a full case conceptualization spanning concerns, risk factors, supportive factors, treatment considerations, and aftercare recommendations, completed before full licensure. The position states its own rationale: rather than relying on raw hour counts, someone experienced evaluates the new provider's actual casework before independent practice. The 18 hours remain valid for up to 12 months after the practicum, and the model mirrors the consultation structure developed at Memoru in Boulder.

Beside the department's proposal

At the August 21 meeting the department showed its own proposal next to the committee's. The prerequisites, the New Mexico module, continuing education, records, and the transition pathway do not differ. What differs:

DIDACTICSame 80-hour total, proportioned differently: the committee puts 78 hours in the general module with 2 of simulated patient work; the department puts 65 in the general module, 5 in a role-specific module, and 10 in simulated patient work spread through the training. At the meeting, Larry Leeman suggested reducing the 10 to about six.
STAGESThe department's practicum reaches 100 or 120 hours through 20 hours of co-facilitation, a 60-hour general practicum of six individual patients and two groups, 20 hours of preparatory and integrative sessions, and, for the provider track, 20 supervisory hours. It carries no well-participants stage: the department stated the statute does not allow practicum work with people who lack a qualifying condition.
CHECKPOINTBoth proposals require two full case presentations before licensure. The committee separates them into the 18-hour consultation group; the department folds them into the practicum hours, presented to mentors and supervisors, and agreed at the meeting that combining individual presentation with a group is better than either alone.
AFTERThe department adds 10 mentorship hours after graduation: an obligation on programs at no added cost, optional for the graduate, enforced through program certification. The committee's position carries none, on the argument that the pre-licensure checkpoint is where the leverage is.
PAYThe committee permits compensation from stage three, with four of the eight medicine sessions before pay; the department permits it after 50 practicum hours, half the base.

How the position evolved

JUL 17Dr. Anne Metz presented the structure to the committee: rename the permit, a single didactic standard, a staged practicum with a lawful path to client contact, and consultation sign-off tied to two presented cases.
JUL 27The summary of the recommended rules went to the department: 80 didactic hours, a staged practicum of 60 or 70 hours by role, and 20 hours of case presentation and consultation. It remains the committee's filing of record, and is not published on this site.
AUG 21The refined position above was presented at the committee meeting: the practicum grew to 102 or 114 hours with compensation and medicine-session rules, the consultation settled at 18 hours inside the total, and the nine content-area minimums were stated. No vote was taken.
