New Mexico medical psilocybin program · 7.35.2 NMAC

Where things stand

Created September 7, 2026. Every claim on this page cites the adopted text, the proposed amendments, or a filed document.

New Mexico's rule for psilocybin producers and testing laboratories, 7.35.2 NMAC, has been in effect since June 23, 2026. The Department of Health noticed it on March 24, heard it on April 24, and published it as adopted in the New Mexico Register on June 23. On August 25 the department proposed amendments to three of its sections, the definitions, the general producer requirements, and the transportation of psilocybin, and fixed a rule hearing on them for October 2, 2026, the same hearing that takes up the proposed 7.35.3 NMAC. This Part of the site holds the adopted text, what it requires of the people it reaches, the proposed amendments where they would land, and the record.

Where things stand

OPENThe department published proposed amendments to 7.35.2.7, 7.35.2.10, and 7.35.2.24 on August 25, with the notice fixing a rule hearing for October 2, 9:00 AM, in the Harold Runnels Building auditorium in Santa Fe, in person, by video conference, and by telephone. The amendments would add twenty-one defined terms, let producers sell to healing centers, and let certified practitioners and healing centers transport psilocybin. Each is noted at the section it changes on The adopted rule. Written comment must reach the department by the close of the hearing; how comment works is on Comment.

What the August 25 amendments would change

7Twenty-one defined terms added, the standalone Guide definition struck, Practitioner rewritten, Satchet corrected to sachet. Noted at 7.35.2.7.
10Producers may sell to other producers, healing centers, and practitioners; the adopted text stops at producers and practitioners. Noted at 7.35.2.10.
24Transport by contractors of a certified healing center as well as of a permittee or certified practitioner; the person receiving a shipment verifies the chain of custody form. Noted at 7.35.2.24.

The adopted rule, 7.35.2 NMACCurrent state of truth

The adopted rule, published in the New Mexico Register on June 23, 2026 and in effect from that day: twenty-seven sections governing producer and laboratory permits, premises, allowed products, traceability, the five required tests and their action levels, packaging and the product information document, wastage, transportation with a chain of custody, monitoring, and discipline. It supersedes the text proposed on March 24 and heard on April 24, 2026. Proposed amendments to three of its sections were published August 25, 2026 and go to a hearing on October 2; until adopted, the text here stands unamended.
