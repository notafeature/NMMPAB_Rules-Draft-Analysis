Comment

Comment is taken and recorded at the rule hearing, anticipated for October 2, and the department has said comment continues from publication of the revised rules through the hearing and after it. This page holds the community input channel this site operates, and what happens to what you send.

Updated July 27, 2026.

The official channel

The rule hearing on 7.35.3 NMAC was set for August 28, 2026 when the rule was published. At the August 21 committee meeting the department stated that it will publish revised proposed rules on August 25 and that the hearing is anticipated for October 2, 2026, virtual and in person; the final notice, in preparation with the hearing officer, will fix the date. Comment given at the hearing becomes part of the rulemaking record the department must consider, and the department said on August 21 that public comment continues after publication, through the hearing, during which changes can still be made. The hearing notice, when this site holds it, will be on the record page.

What is at issue

Three things in the published text are unsettled or contested. Each has a page stating what the record holds.

HOURSThe practicum and didactic hours were deferred to the Training and Education Committee by a 7-0 board vote on July 17 and published unchanged on July 23. The committee's recommendation reached the department on July 27, and the department decides what enters the hearing text. The two positions are set out on The committee recommendation.
CS NUMBERThe published text requires a certifying clinician to hold a New Mexico controlled-substance number, at 7.35.3.9 (D)(2) and 7.35.3.8 (B)(3). The department kept the requirement on July 17. The arguments made on the record, on both sides, are on The controlled-substance number.
DEFECTSFive provisions of the published text do not work as written. Each is noted at the provision it sits in, on The published rule.

The input form

Recommendations and resources sent through this form are compiled and forwarded to the Department of Health as one package, and may be shared with a board member who asks to receive them. The form asks for one thing: a short description of what you are sending. Contact details are optional.

How submissions are used

Submissions are reviewed before anything is passed along or published. Anything published on this site is de-identified unless you checked the box above. Publication of a comment records it rather than endorses it. Your email is used only to follow up on what you sent. Public comment already given at meetings is part of the record, in the meeting transcripts.

Community input received

This page is the log of what the form has received. Submissions are published here after review, de-identified by default; a name appears only with the submitter's consent, and an email is never published. Public comment given at a meeting is part of that meeting's record, and is linked here rather than repeated.

JUL 17Training hours, didactic hour requirement. Received through the input form. "The 35 hour didactic requirement seems insufficient. I hope there will be more time for a thoughtful discussion and a rationale before the number of didactic hours is set. An official training and education committee intercession work group would be helpful in pulling together all of the different ideas that have been discussed informally. Then a clear recommendation could be brought to the advisory board." From Kate Hawke. The training-hours record also carries it, with the rest of the community input on the hours.
JUL 17Training hours, facilitator training standards. Public comment to the July 17 board meeting, from Amy Wong-Hope: four points on what should define a credible training program, and a framing on safety. It lives with the meeting record, in full, on the training-hours record.

The document this page is read from

July 23, 2026

Published proposed ruleCurrent state of truth

The department's proposed rule as published July 23, set aside with its August 28 hearing and standing as the last published text; the department's stated date for revised rules is August 25, with the hearing anticipated for early October. First version with final section numbering, 7.35.3.1 through .28. Raised the shared didactic module from 25 hours to 30 and added three curriculum topics. Carried the 100 and 120 practicum hours forward unchanged, six days after the board voted 7-0 to send those hours back to committee. Wrote both waiver dates as December 31, 2027. Added a rule that the practicum may not begin before half the didactic and all simulated patient hours are complete.

This document supersedes three earlier ones. Each of them, what it is, what superseded it and when, and a download, is in the register at Meetings and filings.
