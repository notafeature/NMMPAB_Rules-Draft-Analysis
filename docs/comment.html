Public comment

Comment on the proposed rule is taken and recorded at the August 28 rule hearing, and the department has said input is wanted before then. This page holds the community input channel this site operates, and what happens to what you send.

Updated July 26, 2026.

The official channel

The rule hearing on 7.35.3 NMAC is scheduled for August 28, 2026. Comment given at the hearing becomes part of the rulemaking record the department must consider. On July 17 the department said the published text is what the hearing runs on, and that suggestions on the training hours are needed sooner rather than later. The hearing notice, when this site holds it, will be on the record page.

The input form

Recommendations and resources sent through this form are compiled and forwarded to the Department of Health as one package, and may be shared with a board member who asks to receive them. The form asks for one thing: a short description of what you are sending. Contact details are optional.

How submissions are used

Submissions are reviewed before anything is passed along or published. Anything published on this site is de-identified unless you checked the box above. Publication of a comment records it rather than endorses it. Your email is used only to follow up on what you sent. Public comment already given at meetings is part of the record, in the meeting transcripts.
