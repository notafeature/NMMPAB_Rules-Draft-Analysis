The chain

JUL 23The department published the proposed rule and the hearing was set for August 28. The practicum and didactic hours appear unchanged from July 9; the shared didactic module rose from 25 hours to 30. The published text · typeset on this site
JUL 17The Advisory Board voted 7-0 to send the practicum and didactic hours, with the definitions, to the Training and Education Committee. The department kept the certifying-clinician controlled-substance number and said a hearing would come at the end of August. In the afternoon, Dr. Anne Metz presented her recommendations to the committee, and the department asked for input sooner rather than later, with no deadline. Board transcript · Committee transcript. Both are unofficial and carry no speaker labels.
JUL 16The End-of-Life committee raised specialization as an endorsement layered on a core permit, with end-of-life care as the worked example. Nothing in the published rule creates one.
JUL 9The Advisory Board resolved reciprocity without objection, extending both waiver deadlines to December 31, 2027, and voted to table the practicum and didactic hours for the committee. The access-point role was renamed certifying clinician, and New Mexico EMT licensure was added beside BLS and CPR. The July 9 draft · Transcript, speaker-tagged
JUN 26The Advisory Board voted 3-2 to accept the committee recommendation and submit it to the department, setting aside the controlled-substance number, the practicum hours, and the reciprocity timeline.
JUN 25The department presented a rules-style draft of the training subject to the committee. The June 25 draft
JUN 12The Training and Education Committee issued its recommendation, the origin document of this rulemaking. The recommendation
JAN – MAYThe committee met five times to develop the recommendation: January 23, March 6, April 10, May 8 jointly with the End-of-Life committee, and May 22.
