New Mexico medical psilocybin program · 7.35.3 NMAC

Overview

Reviewed July 27, 2026. Every claim on this site cites the published rule, a named transcript, or a filed document.

New Mexico is writing the rule that governs how medical psilocybin providers are trained and certified. The Department of Health published the proposed text on July 23, 2026, and the rule hearing is August 28. The practicum and didactic hours went to the Training and Education Committee, and the committee's recommendation was submitted on July 27. This site holds the text, the recommendation, and the record of how both came to be.

Where things stand

OPENPracticum and didactic hours, deferred to the Training and Education Committee by a 7-0 board vote on July 17 and published unchanged. The committee's recommendation was submitted July 27: 80 didactic hours, a staged practicum of 60 or 70 hours by role, and 20 hours of case presentation and consultation. The department decides what enters the hearing text.
DEFECTFive provisions of the published text do not work as written; each is noted at the provision it sits in.
SETTLEDThe certifying-clinician controlled-substance number, kept July 17, and the reciprocity deadlines, set to December 31, 2027 on July 9. Both stand in the published text.
