7.35 NMAC · Medical Psilocybin

New Mexico Department of Health · Title 7, Chapter 35 NMAC

The parts of 7.35 NMAC

Each Part of Chapter 35 is one rule. This site holds each published Part, its record, and what it requires of the people it reaches. The statute the rules implement is the Medical Psilocybin Act, Sections 26-2D-1 through 26-2D-11 NMSA 1978, held here as enacted.

Not published

No other Part of Chapter 35 has been published. The Medical Psilocybin Act directs the department to establish qualifying conditions, training, treatment protocols, safety protocols, best practices, and data collection (Section 26-2D-7 NMSA 1978), and to promulgate rules for the collection of data from producers, clinicians, and qualified patients for an annual assessment of the program (Section 26-2D-9 NMSA 1978). No published Part carries the assessment reporting of Section 26-2D-9.
