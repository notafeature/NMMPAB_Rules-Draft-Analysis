What this site is

An independent community record of New Mexico's Training and Education rulemaking for the medical psilocybin program, 7.35.3 NMAC. It exists so that a person affected by the rule can know, at any moment, what the text says, what is still being decided, and how it came to be this way, without attending a meeting or reading a transcript. It serves board and committee members, providers and educators, people preparing to comment at the hearing, and anyone following the process. When the rulemaking ends, it remains as the account of how the rule was made.

Method

Quoted text is verbatim from the source, with line breaks collapsed to single spaces and nothing else altered. Every claim about what the rule requires cites the published text by section, and every claim about what was said or decided cites a named meeting transcript. Where a transcript carries no speaker labels, which is true of both July 17 transcripts, a speaker is named only where the surrounding text fixes the identity, and the basis is stated. Where this site and a primary source disagree, the primary source is right.

The site is built with AI assistance from the public meeting record and the published documents, and reviewed against the sources before publication. Unofficial transcripts are auto-generated and may contain errors; they are labeled as such wherever they are relied on.

Corrections

Errors are corrected in place, and the correction is recorded in the repository history behind this site, which is public. To report one, use the public comment page and choose "An error on this site."
